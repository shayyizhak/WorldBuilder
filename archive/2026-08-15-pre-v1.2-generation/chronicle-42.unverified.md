The Griwick Compact spent twenty years in a state of violent contraction, beginning with external aggression and ending with internal collapse. Its standing fell from very low to nothing, marked by two places rising against it and a succession of rulers killed in their own seat. The power survived three years of war with the Wurn League, losing one battle and winning two, but its internal cohesion failed as three of its four rulers were murdered by their own people.

In 4, famine at Vea Lode drove the Compact to raid Kebarrow, carrying off 43 grain and killing 28. It declared war on the Wurn League in 5 for Threi Cut, a conflict that lasted three years. The Wurn League defeated the Compact at Threi Cut in 5, killing 45, but the Compact reversed this in 6 and 7, beating the Wurn League at Threi Cut in both years and taking the place. During the war, the Compact’s raid on Hadale in 7 was beaten off. After peace in 8, the Compact’s standing deteriorated; Threi Cut rose against it in 13 when its standing had fallen very low, and Vea Lode rose against it in 15 when its standing had fallen to nothing.

The seat passed through four rulers. Pouldrir Ho held the seat since 1 and was killed in 20 by Math Ham. Math Ham contested the claim of Heillvar Maer, the named heir, in 20, but Heillvar took the seat and Math Ham was cast out. Heillvar was killed in 21 by Turaer Danpa, and Befu Seirn took the seat by the strongest claim. Befu was killed in 23 by Bu Rumpirn, and Turaer Danpa took the seat by the strongest claim. The Compact sent six raids: three carried off plunder (Kebarrow in 4, Laehiford in 12, Kebarrow in 17) and three were beaten off (Hedale in 7, Meigate in 13, Hadale in 22). It suffered three raids: one on Griwick in 6 by the Wurn League, which carried off 33 grain and killed 45, and two beaten off (Griwick in 13 by the Kebarrow Compact, Vea Lode in 19 by the Kebarrow Compact). The period ended in 23 with Turaer Danpa still holding the seat.


## The Vea Lode Covenant, 49–51

The Vea Lode Covenant entered the period as a power defined by its succession by election and its control of Vea Lode. Its standing was high enough to declare war, yet fragile enough that the death of its ruler could unravel its authority. The period began with the return of Herpeim Raern, who took service with the Covenant after exile, and ended with the site of Threi Cut rising against it as its standing had fallen to nothing. The Covenant’s trajectory was shaped by a war against the Sworn Men of Meigate and the violent end of its leadership.

In year 49, the Covenant declared war on the Sworn Men of Meigate over a long-standing grievance. It defeated the Sworn Men at Meigate, killing 149, and continued to press the conflict. In year 50, the Covenant defeated the Sworn Men again at Meigate, killing 124, and took the settlement from them. The Sworn Men of Meigate was finished, its last 12 followers scattering. The Covenant’s internal stability broke when Keithfal Naell, its ruler, was killed by Tor Nathgoull at Vea Lode. Herpeim Raern took the seat of the Covenant by election in the same year.

The Covenant’s authority collapsed in year 51. Threi Cut rose against it, exploiting the fact that its standing had fallen to nothing. The Hadale Commune’s raid on Meigate was beaten off, but the Covenant could not hold its remaining territories. The war with the Sworn Men of Meigate ended in peace after two years, but the Covenant itself was left diminished, its power broken by the loss of its ruler and the subsequent uprising.


## The rule of Wuldweald Valdrith over the Kebarrow Compact, 51–51

- `unsupported-manner` — prose says 'seized', which would need a record of 'coup'; there is none

Wuldweald Valdrith seized the seat of the Kebarrow Compact in 51, having contested the claim of the named heir Hehum Skul in an election. The contest followed the killing of Paernmel Has, the previous ruler, by Wuldweald at Kebarrow earlier that year. Wuldweald’s victory set aside Hehum’s claim, and Hehum was cast out of the Kebarrow Compact.

The period was brief, ending in 51 with Wuldweald Valdrith holding the seat. During this time, Breis Thrind was born to Paernmel Has at Kebarrow.
