The existence of Threi Cut in year 1 established a site that would later provoke conflict. In year 24, the Sworn Men of Laehiford demanded tribute from the Griwick Compact and were refused. This refusal triggered a chain of internal instability within the Sworn Men of Laehiford. In year 32, Sou Dra began conspiring against Math Ham, the leader of the Sworn Men of Laehiford. After five years, the conspiracy was uncovered in year 37, leading to Sou Dra’s expulsion from the faction for plotting against its seat.

The removal of Sou Dra created a vacuum that Paernrom Sir exploited. In year 40, Paernrom Sir began conspiring against Thurnean Kourn, the new leader of the Sworn Men of Laehiford. The plot was uncovered two years later in year 42, resulting in Paernrom Sir’s expulsion. This loss of legitimacy prompted Threi Cut to rise against the Sworn Men of Laehiford in year 42. Desperate, the faction spent silver to buy goodwill in year 43, but the gesture was rejected. The weakened position allowed Raneith Meispol to challenge and defeat Thurnean Kourn in year 44, seizing the seat through a coup.

Raneith Meispol’s rule was short-lived due to shifting alliances. In year 47, Voudreirn Wer won Leimmil Theall away from the ruler of the Sworn Men of Laehiford. Exploiting this isolation, Sou Dra had Raneith Meispol murdered at Laehiford in year 49. His death triggered a contest for leadership between Sothkel Sald and Lethsel Troldmirn under the rule of the strongest. In year 49, Lethsel Troldmirn took three places to form the Sworn Men of Hadale, dividing the original faction. This division spurred Heth Fal to begin conspiring against Sothkel Sald in year 50. However, Heth Fal’s designs came to nothing because Sothkel Sald was already dead.
