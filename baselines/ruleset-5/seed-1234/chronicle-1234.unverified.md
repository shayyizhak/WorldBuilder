The Trostead Compact endured nine years of escalating isolation, beginning with external aggression and ending in total dissolution. At the start of the period, the Compact was a power capable of projecting force, as shown by its raid on Micrag in 22, which carried off 28 grain and killed 48. However, its military efforts were increasingly met with resistance; its raid on Geirnpei Shafts in 23 was beaten off, and it suffered a raid on Trostead in 24 from the Galweall League, which carried off 31 grain and killed 25. The internal stability of the Compact was fragile, marked by the murder of Vun Doultheam by Tha Vu in 25, an act that triggered the succession of Besveir Maleal by right of birth.

The seat passed twice in this period, first to Besveir Maleal and then to Del Monbirn, who won an open challenge in 27. Besveir Maleal was cast out, and Tha Vu was cast out in 27 for a conspiracy uncovered after three years. In 28, a marriage between Keandtei Taegro and Thimseild Soldrind bound the Compact to the Galweall League, but this alliance did not prevent conflict. The Compact’s raid on Gaehollow in 28 was beaten off, and in 29, the Galweall League declared war over a long-standing grievance, breaking the alliance and defeating the Compact at Trostead with 167 dead.

The Compact lost Trostead to the Galweall League in 29 and was finished, its last 7 followers scattering. The period ended with peace between the Galweall League and the Compact after one year of war, following the collapse of the Compact. Throughout the nine years, the Compact fought one battle, which it lost, and sent three raids, two of which were beaten off. Its rule ended with Del Monbirn, whose house ended, leaving the power destroyed and landless.

The Galweall League’s decade was defined by the fragility of its rule and the persistence of its enemies. Its standing fell to nothing in 42, prompting Geirnpei Shafts to rise against it, yet it survived to defeat House Buldbei at Geirnpei Shafts later that year. The League fought two wars, against the Rising of Pellweagate and House Buldbei, winning both battles and taking two places. Its internal stability was nonexistent; ten people held the seat in ten years, with an average tenure of less than one year. The period ended with the League still holding the seat, but under a new ruler who had taken it by coup, having killed his predecessor.

Lan Thraestun began the period holding the seat, but after losing an open challenge to Del Monbirn, he killed Monbirn at Themoor. Thraestun was then killed by Dasould Ream at Themoor. Laelldou Nerum took the seat by setting aside the named heir Haeldkeind Nen. Nerum raided Pellweagate, killing 31 but taking nothing, before being killed by Dasould Ream at Themoor. Veith Hei took the seat by setting aside the named heir Hibrei Fou. Hei declared war on the Rising of Pellweagate, breaking their alliance, and was killed by Besveir Maleal at Themoor. The League defeated the Rising of Pellweagate at Pellweagate, taking the settlement and finishing the power. Haeldkeind Nen took the seat by election.

Nond Mea took the seat from Haeldkeind Nen after winning an open challenge, casting Nen out. Keandtei Taegro’s conspiracy against Nond Mea was uncovered, and Taegro was executed. Vaswei Grand’s conspiracy was uncovered, and Grand was cast out. Rusund Gilteil challenged Nond Mea and won, taking the seat by coup and killing Nond Mea at Themoor. Was Leithwon had Gilteil murdered at Themoor. Varnnael Wansen took the seat by setting aside the named heir Peansith Rae. Rend Greall had Wansen murdered at Themoor. Mound Sith took the seat by election. Greimfourn Goul challenged Mound Sith, won, and took the seat, killing Goul at Themoor. Fear Mel challenged Goul, won, and took the seat by coup. The League defeated House Buldbei at Pei Delve, taking the place.
