## House Thream, 2–29

- `outside-the-window` — …The era opened with the death of Freamteas Kraeth, who had held the seat since year 1, an…… — the record opens in 2 and holds nothing from year 1

House Thream began the period as a power bound to the Kraeford Compact by alliance, but its trajectory was defined by a succession crisis and a subsequent war with the Goummeidale Compact that ended its existence. The period saw six people hold the seat, with the power losing two key settlements and ultimately being finished, its land scattered. The era opened with the death of Freamteas Kraeth, who had held the seat since year 1, and the rise of Bran Nas, who took the seat by the strongest claim. Bran Nas ruled until his death in year 6, during which time House Thream fought a war against the Goummeidale Compact over Sti Seam. The Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream twice at Sti Seam, taking the settlement in year 6.

Following Bran Nas’s death, Kokaes Threll took the seat by the strongest claim, setting aside the named heir Ma Graltaeld, who was cast out. Kokaes Threll’s rule was short; he was murdered by Fous Bo in year 8. Vinbeil Real then took the seat by the strongest claim. Vinbeil Real ruled until his death in year 17. During his tenure, House Thream’s raids were largely unsuccessful, with six raids beaten off and one raid on Goummeidale also beaten off. The only raid that carried off plunder was the one on Seithfaehollow in year 5, before Vinbeil Real’s rise. Sen Tom took the seat in year 17 after Vinbeil Real’s death, ruling until he was challenged and killed by Maethhol Gondrur in year 26.

The final phase of House Thream was marked by renewed conflict with the Goummeidale Compact. The Goummeidale Compact declared war in year 27 over a long-standing grievance and defeated House Thream at Loufell in years 27 and 28, taking the settlement in year 28. House Thream was finished in year 28, its last three followers scattering. The period ended with the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream making peace, though the power no longer existed. Throughout the 28 years, House Thream suffered two famines, killing 37 people, and lost two places to the Goummeidale Compact. The quarrel with the Goummeidale Compact resulted in four battles, all lost by House Thream.


## The rule of Maethhol Gondrur over the Goummeidale Compact, 40–51

- `unsupported-manner` — prose says 'seized', which would need a record of 'coup'; there is none
- `ambiguous-short-name` — two powers here are called "compact"; the short form says neither
- `out-of-order` — the killing of Brethneld Draer in 40 is what opened the seat, and is told after Maethhol Gondrur takes it

Maethhol Gondrur took the seat of the Goummeidale Compact in 40 after Seill Kreannae killed Brethneld Draer. The new ruler’s tenure began with a plague at Seithfaehollow that killed 106 people and lasted one year. A second outbreak in 42 took 103 lives, followed by a third wave in 43 that killed 150 and drove 171 others from the settlement before burning out after two years. During this time, Maethhol’s son Hidol Krund was born at Loufell.

Internal stability eroded as conspiracies were uncovered. Seill Kreannae, who had previously seized power, was cast out in 42 for a plot against the seat. In 46, Thaeld Faeth was executed for a conspiracy that had lasted three years. That same year, the Compact spent 185 silver to buy back goodwill after Thaeld Faeth won Fol Skeambol away from the ruler. Gu Veam, raised to heir in 44, attempted to kill Maethhol in 47 and was cast out, later declared an outlaw in 50 for a separate conspiracy. Skein Teththeald, also raised to steward, won Meind Veild away from the ruler in 50 and was cast out that year for his own conspiracy.

The ruler’s standing collapsed in 50 when Sti Seam rose against the Goummeidale Compact, whose standing had fallen to nothing. Maethhol Gondrur’s rule ended in 51 with the period closing. During the final years, he fathered two more children, Pan Dros and Fraethrul Fri, at Loufell.
