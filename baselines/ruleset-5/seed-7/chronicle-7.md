# A history of the Rulllound Vale

Seed 7, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Goummeidale Compact's Grudge: the Goummeidale Compact against House Thream, 5–7

In year 5, the Goummeidale Compact declared war on House Thream over the site of Sti Seam. The conflict began with a defeat for House Thream at Sti Seam, where 34 people died. The Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream again at the same site in year 6, resulting in 28 deaths. Following this second defeat, the Goummeidale Compact took Sti Seam from House Thream.

The war lasted for two years. In year 7, the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream made peace. Both sides were exhausted.

### The Long Quarrel over Kraeford: the Goummeidale Compact against the Kraeford Compact, 21–23

The Goummeidale Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact in 21 over a long-standing grievance. In that same year, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, where 110 people died, and took the settlement from the Kraeford Compact. The conflict continued into 22, when the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, resulting in 181 deaths, and subsequently took Kraeford from the Kraeford Compact.

The war ended in 23 after two years of fighting. The Goummeidale Compact and the Kraeford Compact made peace following the collapse of the Kraeford Compact.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 2–21

The Goummeidale Compact began the period holding Ra Cut, a site no one else held, and ended it with Seithfaehollow, having taken it from the Kraeford Compact. Four people held the seat over the twenty years, each ruling for an average of 4.7 years. The power fought three battles, winning all of them, and sent two raids that both carried off plunder. Its standing fell very low twice, causing Ra Cut and Sti Seam to rise against it. The period closed with Freindmald Beindlea still holding the seat.

Sealoll Lael held the seat at the start, binding the Compact to the Kraeford Compact through the marriage of Kaell Frouspim and Frouldtoun Dror, and to House Thream through the marriage of Freindmald Beindlea and Fous Bo. The Compact declared war on House Thream for Sti Seam, breaking the alliance with Thream. It defeated Thream at Sti Seam in year 5 and took the site in year 6. Vinbeil Real’s conspiracy against Sealoll Lael was uncovered, and Vinbeil was cast out. Sealoll Lael was murdered by Thral Rei at Goummeidale, and Fous Bo took the seat by right of birth. The Kraeford Compact raided Goummeidale, carrying off 34 grain and killing 55.

Fous Bo’s rule was marked by Thral Rei’s conspiracy, which led to Thral Rei being cast out. Ra Cut rose against the Compact as its standing fell very low. Fous Bo was murdered by Kraedren Waeld at Goummeidale, and Kaell Frouspim took the seat by right of birth. Kraedren Waeld’s conspiracy was uncovered, and he was cast out. The Compact raided Kraeford and Seithfaehollow, carrying off plunder in both instances. House Thream raided the Compact’s sites, but all raids were beaten off.

Kaell Frouspim’s rule ended when he was killed by Frouldtoun Dror at Goummeidale. The Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact over a long-standing grievance, breaking the earlier alliance. It defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, taking the site. Freindmald Beindlea took the seat by right of birth. The period ended with Freindmald Beindlea holding the seat, the Compact having expanded its territory but lost three rulers to internal murder.

### The Kraeford Compact, 2–23

The Kraeford Compact’s twenty-two years ended in its destruction, a trajectory defined by internal instability and a final, fatal war with the Goummeidale Compact. The period began with the Compact bound to House Thream by alliance, but its leadership was fragile. Skur Stas, who had held the seat since before the period, was killed by Kraedren Waeld in year 5. This act triggered a succession contest decided by election, which Stearnke Gullleith won, rejecting Waeld’s claim. Waeld was cast out, then declared outlaw after a conspiracy against Gullleith was uncovered. Gullleith ruled until his death in year 9, passing the seat by election to Frouldtoun Dror.

Dror’s rule saw the Compact’s military efforts fail repeatedly. Of the five raids it sent out, only one succeeded, carrying off plunder from Goummeidale in year 12; the other four, including attacks on Kreagemoor, Ra Cut, and Sti Seam, were beaten off. The Compact suffered three raids itself, losing grain and lives at Seithfaehollow and Kraeford. Internal cohesion eroded further when Thral Rei returned from exile in year 16, only to be executed for conspiracy against Dror in year 18. Two famines struck Seithfaehollow and Kraeford in years 14 and 15, killing 74 people. In year 21, the Goummeidale Compact declared war over a long-standing grievance, breaking its alliance with the Compact.

The war proved decisive. The Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow in year 21 and at Kraeford in year 22, taking both places. The Kraeford Compact was finished, its land lost and its last two followers scattering. Peace was made between the Goummeidale Compact and the remnants of the Kraeford Compact in year 23. Throughout the period, three people held the seat, with rules ending by death, house ending, and murder. The Compact fought two battles, losing both to the Goummeidale Compact.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 22–41

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by internal bloodshed and the elimination of external rivals. The period began with the murder of Freindmald Beindlea by Waeld Dould at Goummeidale, an act that triggered a succession crisis resolved only when Frouldtoun Dror took the seat by right of birth. The Compact immediately secured its position by defeating the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, taking the settlement and ending the rival power. It then turned against House Thream, defeating them twice at Loufell in 27 and 28, taking that settlement and finishing House Thream as well.

Internal stability proved fleeting. Frouldtoun Dror was cast out in 28 after an attempted murder was traced back to him, replaced by Voumfaern Ki. The seat passed rapidly thereafter: Skullhein Threill won an open challenge in 29, was murdered by Vasgou Raend in 30, and Theirnpae Gro took the seat by birth. Theirnpae was killed by Rateal Fototh, who had won a challenge, but Vasgou Raend then won a challenge and took the seat by coup. Vasgou Raend died in 34, allowing Wold Risla to take the seat by birth. Wold Risla was cast out in 35 after losing an open challenge to Brethneld Draer, who was murdered in 40 by Seill Kreannae. Maethhol Gondrur took the seat by birth in 40, still holding it at the end of the period.

The Compact’s rule was marked by six internal murders and five castings out. It fought three battles, all won, and sent one raid that took nothing. It suffered three raids, all beaten off. A plague at Seithfaehollow in 40 killed 106 people. Six people were courted away from its rulers. The quarrel with House Thream persisted through two battles. The period ended with Maethhol Gondrur holding the seat, having survived the violence that characterized the preceding decades.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 42–50

The Goummeidale Compact’s rule in these years was defined by the survival of Maethhol Gondrur against a series of internal conspiracies that eroded its authority until its final collapse. At the start, in 42, the Compact faced a plague at Seithfaehollow that killed 103 people and a conspiracy by Seill Kreannae against Maethhol Gondrur. Kreannae’s plot was uncovered after four years, and he was cast out of the Compact. The plague continued into 43, claiming 150 more lives and driving 171 others to flee Seithfaehollow.

Maethhol Gondrur remained in the seat through further challenges. In 46, Thaeld Faeth’s conspiracy against him was uncovered after three years, leading to Faeth’s execution. In 47, Gu Veam attempted to kill Maethhol Gondrur; the attempt failed, and Veam was cast out. Veam’s standing worsened in 50 when another conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered, resulting in Veam being declared an outlaw.

The period ended with the destruction of the Compact’s power. In 50, Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered after three years, and Teththeald was cast out. With the Compact’s standing fallen to nothing, Sti Seam rose against it in 50. The Compact was finished, its authority extinguished by the rebellion it could no longer suppress.
