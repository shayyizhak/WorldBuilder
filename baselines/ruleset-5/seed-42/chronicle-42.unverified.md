Five individuals held the seat during these twenty years, with the position passing between them four times. Reweld Wul took the seat by election following Sisrill’s death, but his rule ended when Witrou Breim had him murdered in year 14. Witrou Breim then assumed the seat by election, only to be challenged and cast out by Pouldrir Ho in year 15. The Kebarrow Compact’s nineteen-year tenure from 22 to 40 was defined by a relentless, losing war with the Griwick Compact and a succession of internal purges that left its leadership fractured. The period began with Sou Dra holding the seat, a position he had held since 20, but his rule was immediately undermined by conspiracy and external pressure. The Compact’s standing eroded through four battles, all lost to the Griwick Compact, and the loss of two key places, Meigate and Kebarrow itself. By the end, the power was finished, its land seized and its followers scattered.

Sou Dra’s rule was marked by paranoia and violence. In 25, his raid on Griwick was beaten off, and his conspiracy against him by Turaer Danpa was uncovered after six years, leading to Danpa’s execution. The Compact’s aggression continued in 26, raiding Laehiford and carrying off 44 grain, which prompted the Griwick Compact to raid Kebarrow in return. In 27, Sou Dra faced an open challenge from Tor Nathgoull, who won the seat. Sou Dra was cast out, ending his rule. During this transition, the Compact suffered a famine at Meigate that killed 34 people. Theald Va’s conspiracy against Sou Dra was also uncovered in 28, resulting in his execution.

Tor Nathgoull’s rule was brief and unstable. He took the seat in 29 after winning an open challenge, but his authority was contested. In 32, Throll Kell challenged him openly and won, taking the seat by coup. Tor Nathgoull was cast out. Throll Kell’s rule was similarly short-lived; in 35, Beas Krouthea challenged him openly and won, casting Throll Kell out. Beas Krouthea’s rule ended in 38 when Kou Peis murdered him at Kebarrow. During this time, the Compact’s military position collapsed. It fought four battles against the Griwick Compact, all lost, with 57, 48, 142, and 118 dead respectively. The Griwick Compact took Kebarrow in 39, and the Kebarrow Compact was finished, its last 9 followers scattering.
