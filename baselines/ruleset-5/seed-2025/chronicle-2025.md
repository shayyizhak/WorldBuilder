# A history of the Foldkal Vale

Seed 2025, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The Thea Seam War: the Waeslefell Compact against the Baesveireach Compact, 4–10

The war began in year 4 when the Waeslefell Compact declared war on the Baesveireach Compact for control of Thea Seam. In that same year, the Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam, leaving 26 dead. The conflict continued at the same site through year 9, with the Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeating the Waeslefell Compact in each subsequent year. The casualties in these engagements decreased over time: 20 dead in year 5, 13 in year 6, 11 in year 7, 9 in year 8, and 8 in year 9.

The fighting ended in year 10 when the Waeslefell Compact and the Baesveireach Compact made peace after six years of war. Both sides were exhausted by the end of the conflict.

## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### The Deafil League, 2–35

The Deafil League spent thirty-four years bound to the Baesveireach Compact through four marriages, yet ended its existence defeated by the Waeslefell Compact. At the start, the League’s standing was secured by marital ties to Baesveireach in years 2, 3, and 7. These alliances did not prevent conflict; the Waeslefell Compact raided Kremoor in year 8, prompting the Deafil League to raid Waeslefell in retaliation. The League’s raids continued with varying success: it carried off plunder from Waeslefell in year 8 and Weisfaewick in year 9, but its raid on Fa Cut in year 10 got through without taking anything. The League suffered raids from the Waeslefell Compact on Kremoor in year 8, Skomere in year 9, Kremoor in year 18, and Skomere in year 19.

Internal instability marked the middle of the period. Feasleith Husbill died in year 11, and Steapall Faedild took the seat by election, setting aside the named heir Bage Busba. Faedild’s rule ended when Fur Roundpoull challenged him openly in year 23 and won. Fur Roundpoull kept the seat after defeating an open challenge from Duthsourn Tae in year 28, but Sealdvern Geitoun took the seat by coup later that same year. Sealdvern’s rule was brief; he was cast out in year 29 after an attempt on Ground Raeskur failed. Deththou Keis took the seat by election in year 29, setting aside the named heir Touthseand Gourses. During this time, the League’s standing fell very low, leading Kremoor to rise against it in year 24 and break away as Free Kremoor in year 28.

The League’s end came through war with the Waeslefell Compact. In year 27, Sealdvern Geitoun married Sundsea Pim, binding the League to the Waeslefell Compact, but the Compact declared war in year 34 over a long-standing grievance. The Compact defeated the League at Skomere in year 34, killing 179, and took the settlement. The Deafil League was finished in year 34, landless, with its last four followers scattering. Peace was made between the powers in year 35. Throughout the period, five people held the seat, with rules ending in three cast-outs, one death, and one house ending. The League fought one battle, which it lost, and sent eight raids, five of which were beaten off.

### The Baesveireach Compact, 4–30

The Baesveireach Compact entered the period as a military power defined by its succession by strongest, immediately drawn into a war with the Waeslefell Compact over Thea Seam. The conflict began in year 4 when the Waeslefell Compact declared war and broke its alliance with Baesveireach. Baesveireach and its allies won seven successive battles at Thea Seam between years 4 and 9, inflicting 87 deaths on the Waeslefell Compact. The war ended in year 10 with peace, leaving Baesveireach exhausted but intact. During the war, Baesveireach sent five raids; two carried off plunder from Waeslefell and Weisfaewick, while three were beaten off.

Internal stability proved fragile. In year 9, Hou Lae’s conspiracy against the ruler Sildbeld Bouthwoun was uncovered, leading to Hou Lae’s execution. Bouthwoun died in year 13, triggering a succession contest decided by strongest. Kramboul Dend took the seat, setting aside the named heir Fur Roundpoull, who was cast out. Dend’s rule ended in year 18 when he died. Bage Busba took the seat by the strongest claim. The Compact’s standing fell very low by year 16, when Thea Seam rose against it. A plague broke out in Baesveireach in year 21, killing 213 people, followed by further deaths and flight in years 22 and 23.

In year 25, Steapall Faedild returned from exile and took service with the Compact. The Waeslefell Compact declared war again in year 26 over a long-standing grievance. Baesveireach lost three battles in years 27, 28, and 29, losing Thea Seam and then Baesveireach itself to the Waeslefell Compact. Steapall Faedild had Bage Busba murdered in year 28 and took the seat, setting aside the named heir Drourn Nethi, who was cast out. Steapall was killed by Ground Raeskur in year 29. The period ended in year 30 with peace between the powers, after the collapse of the Baesveireach Compact.

### The Waeslefell Compact, 22–41

The Waeslefell Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by violent succession and a shifting balance of power. The period began with Vaell Foull, who had held the seat since 17, and ended with Nounmam Mith, who took the seat in 37 and remained there. Six people held the seat in total, with an average tenure of 3.1 years. The seat passed from one person to another five times. The rule of the Compact was marked by internal instability, with two rulers cast out, two killed, and one dying in office.

Ground Raeskur seized the seat in 24 after murdering Vaell Foull at Skomere. His rule was challenged by Staellou Keillthae, whose failed attempt on Ground Raeskur’s life was traced back to the earlier murder. Staellou Keillthae was cast out for attempted murder and later declared an outlaw for conspiracy. Ground Raeskur also ordered the murder of Steapall Faedild at Baesveireach in 29. Vamkei Dridrill’s conspiracy against Ground Raeskur was uncovered, leading to Vamkei Dridrill’s exile. Fur Roundpoull returned from exile to serve the Compact before challenging Ground Raeskur openly in 32. Fur Roundpoull won the challenge and took the seat. Sundsea Pim’s conspiracy against Fur Roundpoull was uncovered, and Sundsea Pim was executed. Fur Roundpoull died in 33.

Rontaen Sin contested the claim of the named heir Rearn Thill and took the seat, setting aside Rearn Thill’s claim. The Compact was divided, with Rearn Thill carrying off two places as Greater Baesveireach. Rontaen Sin was murdered by Nounmam Mith in 36. Deththou Keis took the seat by right of birth. Nounmam Mith challenged Deththou Keis and won, taking the seat by coup. Deththou Keis was cast out. The Compact fought five battles, losing one to the Baesveireach Compact at Thea Seam in 26, but winning four others against the Baesveireach Compact and the Deafil League. It took three places: Thea Seam, Baesveireach, and Skomere. It lost two places to Greater Baesveireach. Thea Seam rose against the Compact in 35, when its standing had fallen to nothing. The period ended with Nounmam Mith holding the seat.

### The Waeslefell Compact, 42–51

The Waeslefell Compact endured a decade of violent instability, beginning with a contested succession and ending with the death of its final ruler. The period was defined by rapid turnover at the seat, internal purges, and the loss of territory. Six people held the seat in ten years, with the average tenure lasting only 1.5 years. The power shifted from one holder to another five times, moving through open challenges, murders, and the setting aside of heirs. By the end, the Compact had lost two places, suffered a plague that killed 176 people, and seen its standing fall to nothing before rising again through marriage.

The period opened in 42 when Sealdvern Geitoun returned from exile to serve the Compact. That same year, Greandsam Kreir challenged Nounmam Mith openly for the seat, won, and cast Mith out. The Compact immediately raided Weisfaewick, carrying off 41 grain and killing 49, which prompted Greater Baesveireach to raid Waeslefell and Skomere; both raids were beaten off. In 47, the internal stability collapsed. Thraebaern Ramdem murdered Greandsam Kreir at Skomere. Sealdvern Geitoun was killed by Feinnind Keitrith at Waeslefell. Noulldou Beivourn contested the claim of the named heir Touthseand Gourses, took the seat, and set aside the heir’s claim. The Compact was divided, with Touthseand Gourses taking two places to form the Skomere Charter.

Woum Teldlei challenged Noulldou Beivourn openly in 48, won, and cast him out. Fa Cut rose against the Compact that year, as its standing had fallen to nothing. Woum Teldlei was killed by Feinnind Keitrith at Waeslefell in 49. Ronith Wasnur contested the claim of the named heir Meirnhaes Kould, took the seat, and cast Kould out. In 50, Seithfum Mim married Til Saeld, binding the Compact to Free Kremoor. Gandkim Kiveald’s attempt on Ronith Wasnur failed, and his subsequent conspiracy was uncovered; he was cast out and declared outlaw. Full Sanweil’s conspiracy against Ronith Wasnur was also uncovered, and he was cast out. Two people were courted away from the ruler during this time.

Ronith Wasnur died at Waeslefell in 51. Free Kremoor raided Fa Cut, but the raid was beaten off. Haeth Tha contested the claim of the named heir Thraebaern Ramdem, took the seat, and cast Ramdem out. The period ended in 51 with Haeth Tha still holding the seat. Throughout the decade, the Compact sent one raid, which carried off plunder, and suffered three raids, all beaten off. One person was murdered from within. Six people were cast out. Two places were lost. Two years of sickness killed 176 people. One marriage tied the Compact to another power.

## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Til Saeld over Free Kremoor, 49–51

Til Saeld assumed the seat of Free Kremoor in 49, following the exile of Nounmam Mith. Mith had been cast out after an attempt on the life of Woum Teldlei failed and was traced to him. With no claimant remaining, Saeld was raised from Kremoor to take the position.

The period ended in 51 when a raid by Free Kremoor on Fa Cut was beaten off.

### The rule of Haeth Tha over the Waeslefell Compact, 51–51

In year 51, Ronith Wasnur, the ruler of the Waeslefell Compact, died in Waeslefell. His death triggered a contest for the succession, which followed the rule of primogeniture. Haeth Tha challenged the claim of Thraebaern Ramdem, the named heir. Haeth Tha took the seat of the Waeslefell Compact, setting aside the claim of Thraebaern Ramdem.

Thraebaern Ramdem was cast out of the Waeslefell Compact as the losing claimant. During this same year, a raid by Free Kremoor on Fa Cut was beaten off. The period of Haeth Tha’s rule ended in year 51.
