# A history of the Rulllound Vale

Seed 7, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Goummeidale Compact's Grudge: the Goummeidale Compact against House Thream, 5–7

In year 5, the Goummeidale Compact declared war on House Thream over the site of Sti Seam. The conflict began immediately, with the Goummeidale Compact defeating House Thream at Sti Seam in the same year, an engagement that left 34 dead. The fighting continued into year 6, where the Goummeidale Compact again defeated House Thream at the same location, resulting in 28 deaths. Following this second defeat, the Goummeidale Compact took Sti Seam from House Thream.

The war lasted for two years. In year 7, the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream made peace. Both sides were exhausted by the conflict.

### The Long Quarrel over Kraeford: the Goummeidale Compact against the Kraeford Compact, 21–23

The Goummeidale Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact in 21 over a long-standing grievance. In that same year, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, where 110 people died, and took the settlement from them. The conflict continued into 22, when the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact again at Kraeford, resulting in 181 deaths and the transfer of that settlement from the Kraeford Compact to the Goummeidale Compact.

The war ended in 23 after two years of fighting. The Goummeidale Compact and the Kraeford Compact made peace following the collapse of the Kraeford Compact.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 2–21

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 2 to 21 was defined by internal collapse and external aggression. The period began with the occupation of Ra Cut and ended with Freindmald Beindlea holding the seat after a succession of violent deaths. Four people held the seat, each ruling for an average of 4.7 years. The power suffered eight raids, lost two places to rebellion, and saw its standing fall so low that settlements rose against it. Despite winning three battles, the Compact was eroded by two wars, a plague in Kreagemoor, and a series of murders within its own leadership.

Sealoll Lael held the seat at the start, ruling by primogeniture. In 3, Kaell Frouspim married Frouldtoun Dror, binding the Compact to the Kraeford Compact. In 5, Freindmald Beindlea married Fous Bo, binding the Compact to House Thream. That same year, the Compact declared war on House Thream over Sti Seam. Vinbeil Real’s conspiracy against Sealoll was uncovered, and Vinbeil was cast out. The Compact defeated House Thream at Sti Seam, killing 34, and defeated them again in 6, killing 28, taking Sti Seam from House Thream. Ma Graltaeld returned from exile in 7. In 9, Kraedren Waeld returned from exile. In 11, Fous Bo returned from exile. The Kraeford Compact’s raid on Kreagemoor was beaten off.

In 12, Thral Rei murdered Sealoll Lael at Goummeidale. Sealoll was killed by Thral Rei. The Kraeford Compact raided Goummeidale, carrying off 34 grain and killing 55. Fous Bo took the seat by right of birth. In 13, the Compact raided Kraeford, carrying off 40 grain and killing 28. Thral Rei’s conspiracy against Fous Bo was uncovered, and Thral Rei was cast out. Ra Cut rose against the Compact. In 14, a plague broke out at Kreagemoor, taking 57. The Kraeford Compact’s raid on Ra Cut was beaten off. In 15, the plague continued, killing 131 and causing 137 to flee. The Kraeford Compact’s raid on Sti Seam was beaten off. In 16, the Compact raided Seithfaehollow, carrying off 32 grain and killing 36. House Thream’s raid on Goummeidale was beaten off.

In 17, the plague at Kreagemoor killed 48 and caused 75 to flee. In 18, House Thream’s raid on Kreagemoor was beaten off. Sti Seam rose against the Compact. In 19, Kraedren Waeld murdered Fous Bo at Goummeidale. Fous Bo was killed by Kraedren Waeld. House Thream’s raid on Sti Seam was beaten off. Kaell Frouspim took the seat by right of birth. In 20, House Thream’s raid on Ra Cut was beaten off. Kraedren Waeld’s conspiracy against Kaell was uncovered, and Kraedren Waeld was cast out. In 21, the Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact. Kaell Frouspim was killed by Frouldtoun Dror at Goummeidale. The Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, killing 110, and took Seithfaehollow from them. Freindmald Beindlea took the seat by right of birth. The period ended with Freindmald Beindlea still holding the seat.

### The Kraeford Compact, 2–23

The Kraeford Compact endured for twenty-two years, beginning with an alliance with House Thream and ending in total destruction at the hands of the Goummeidale Compact. Its history was defined by a succession of three rulers, two of whom held the seat for only a few years before their deaths or the end of their houses. The power fought two battles, both lost to the Goummeidale Compact, and suffered two major famines that killed 74 people. Despite sending five raids, only one succeeded in taking plunder, while the rest were beaten off. The period closed with the Compact finished, its land lost and its followers scattered.

Skur Stas held the seat until year five, when he was killed by Kraedren Waeld at Kraeford. Waeld contested the election for the seat against the named heir, Stearnke Gullleith, but Gullleith took the seat and Waeld was cast out. Waeld’s subsequent conspiracy was uncovered, and he was declared an outlaw. Gullleith ruled until his death in year nine, after which Frouldtoun Dror took the seat by election. During this time, the Compact faced external threats: House Thream raided Seithfaehollow in year five, and the Goummeidale Compact raided Kraeford in year thirteen. The Compact’s own raid on Kreagemoor in year eleven was beaten off.

Frouldtoun Dror’s rule saw the Compact’s decline. Thral Rei returned from exile in year sixteen and served the Compact, but his conspiracy against Dror was uncovered in year eighteen, leading to his execution. The Goummeidale Compact declared war in year twenty-one, defeating the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow and taking the settlement. Dror had Kaell Frouspim murdered at Goummeidale that same year. In year twenty-two, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, taking the settlement and finishing the power. Theirnpae Gro married Wold Risla that year, binding the Compact to House Thream, but the Compact was already destroyed. Peace was made in year twenty-three.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 42–50

The Goummeidale Compact’s rule in these years was defined by the survival of Maethhol Gondrur against a succession of internal conspiracies and the collapse of its territorial control. At the start, the power faced a demographic crisis at Seithfaehollow, where a plague broke out in year 42 and killed 103 people. The same year, Seill Kreannae launched a conspiracy against Gondrur that lasted four years before being uncovered. Kreannae was cast out of the Goummeidale Compact, but the plague at Seithfaehollow continued, killing 150 more and driving 171 to flee in year 43.

Gondrur remained in the seat through year 46, when Thaeld Faeth’s conspiracy against him was uncovered after three years. Faeth was put to death for the attempt. In year 47, Gu Veam made an attempt on Gondrur’s life that failed and was traced back, leading to Veam’s expulsion from the Goummeidale Compact. Veam’s standing deteriorated further in year 50 when his subsequent conspiracy was uncovered; having already left, he was declared an outlaw by the Goummeidale Compact.

The period ended with the destruction of the Goummeidale Compact’s authority. In year 50, Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy against Gondrur was uncovered after three years, and Teththeald was cast out. With the Compact’s standing fallen to nothing, Sti Seam rose against it in year 50. Gondrur held the seat until the power itself was finished by the uprising.


## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Maethhol Gondrur over the Goummeidale Compact, 40–51

Maethhol Gondrur assumed the seat of the Goummeidale Compact in 40 by right of birth, following the murder of the previous ruler, Brethneld Draer, by Seill Kreannae. The period opened with a plague at Seithfaehollow that killed 106 people before burning out after one year. A second outbreak in 42 claimed 103 lives, and a third in 43 killed 150 and drove 171 others to flee before ending after two years. During this time, Maethhol’s son Hidol Krund was born at Loufell, and Skein Teththeald was raised to steward.

Internal stability eroded as conspiracies were uncovered. Seill Kreannae’s plot against Maethhol was revealed in 42, leading to his exile. In 44, Thaeld Faeth won Fol Skeambol away from the ruler, and Gu Veam was named heir. Thaeld’s own conspiracy was uncovered in 46, resulting in his execution. The Compact spent 185 silver to buy back goodwill lost to Skeambol’s defection. Gu Veam’s attempt on Maethhol’s life failed in 47, and he was cast out, later declared an outlaw in 50. Theldsild Tripould was raised to heir in 47, and Thra Meil married her in 48.

The ruler’s standing collapsed in 50 when Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy was uncovered, leading to his exile. Sti Seam rose against the Goummeidale Compact that same year, as the power’s standing had fallen to nothing. Meind Veild was raised to steward in 50. The period ended in 51 with Maethhol Gondrur still holding the seat, having seen the birth of his son Fraethrul Fri and the birth of Thra Meil’s daughter Tea Meir.
