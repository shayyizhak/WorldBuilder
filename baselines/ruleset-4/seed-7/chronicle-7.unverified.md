## House Thream, 2–29

- `wrong-collapse` — …The power lost two places, Sti Seam and Loufell, to the Goummeidale Compact, and was fini…… — nothing here records goummeidale being destroyed

House Thream began the period as a power bound by alliance to the Kraeford Compact, but its trajectory was defined by a succession crisis and a war with the Goummeidale Compact that ended its existence. The period saw six people hold the seat, passing five times, with three dying, two being killed, and one house ending. The power lost two places, Sti Seam and Loufell, to the Goummeidale Compact, and was finished in year 28, landless and scattered.

Freamteas Kraeth died in year 3, and Bran Nas took the seat by the strongest claim. The Goummeidale Compact declared war in year 5 over Sti Seam. House Thream raided Seithfaehollow, carrying off 27 grain and killing 35, but was defeated at Sti Seam with 34 dead. Bran Nas died in year 6. Kokaes Threll contested the heir Ma Graltaeld’s claim and took the seat, casting Ma Graltaeld out. Fous Bo had Kokaes Threll murdered in year 8. Vinbeil Real took the seat by the strongest claim. Fous Bo’s conspiracy was uncovered in year 10, and he was cast out. Vinbeil Real died in year 17.

Sen Tom took the seat in year 17. House Thream sent seven raids, six beaten off and one carrying off plunder. It suffered one raid on Loufell in year 24, which took nothing. Maethhol Gondrur challenged Sen Tom openly in year 26, won, and took the seat. Sen Tom was killed by Maethhol Gondrur in year 26. The Goummeidale Compact declared war in year 27. House Thream was defeated at Loufell in years 27 and 28, with 123 and 103 dead respectively. Loufell was taken in year 28. House Thream was finished in year 28.


## The Goummeidale Compact, 22–41

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by violent succession and the elimination of external rivals. The period began with the murder of Freindmald Beindlea by Waeld Dould, a act that triggered a cascade of internal purges and external conquests. The Compact emerged from the era having destroyed two opposing powers, the Kraeford Compact and House Thream, and securing two territories, Kraeford and Loufell. Its rule passed through ten holders, a turnover marked by frequent assassination and coup, leaving Maethhol Gondrur in possession at the close of the period.

The early years were consumed by the destruction of House Thream and the Kraeford Compact. In 22, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, killing 181, and took the settlement. The Kraeford Compact was finished, its followers scattering. Simultaneously, the Compact fought House Thream, defeating them at Loufell in 27 with 123 dead and again in 28 with 103 dead, taking Loufell and finishing House Thream. These victories were accompanied by internal instability; Waeld Dould was executed for conspiracy in 25, and Frouldtoun Dror was cast out in 28 for an attempted murder. The seat passed to Voumfaern Ki, who was himself challenged and cast out in 29, replaced by Skullhein Threill.

The middle of the period saw Skullhein Threill murdered by Vasgou Raend in 30, leading to a rapid series of coups. Theirnpae Gro took the seat by birth but was killed by Rateal Fototh, who was in turn killed by Vasgou Raend. Vasgou Raend ruled until his death in 34, when Wold Risla succeeded by birth. Wold Risla was challenged and cast out in 35 by Brethneld Draer. Draer’s rule ended in 40 when he was murdered by Seill Kreannae. Throughout these transitions, six people were courted away from the ruler, and six of the Compact’s own people were murdered from within. The period ended with Maethhol Gondrur taking the seat in 40, still holding it as the years closed.
