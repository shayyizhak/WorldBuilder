# A history of the Rendfeall Vale

Seed 1234, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The Long Quarrel over Pei Delve: the Trostead Compact against House Buldbei, 9–11

The Trostead Compact declared war on House Buldbei in year 9, seeking control of Pei Delve. House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Compact at that site later that same year, with 23 dead. The Compact suffered another defeat at Pei Delve in year 10, where 18 died.

The conflict lasted two years. In year 11, the Trostead Compact and House Buldbei made peace, both sides exhausted.

### The War of the Galweall League's Grudge: the Galweall League against the Trostead Compact, 19–21

The Galweall League declared war on the Trostead Compact in 19, citing a long-standing grievance. The Trostead Compact defeated the Galweall League at Geirnpei Shafts in the same year, with 19 dead.

In 20, Lald Neirntean had Kesfound Thrael murdered at Themoor. The Galweall League and its allies defeated the Trostead Compact at Geirnpei Shafts, with 16 dead, and took the site from the Compact.

In 21, the Galweall League and the Trostead Compact made peace after 2 years, both sides exhausted.

### The Trostead Compact, 2–21

The Trostead Compact spent twenty years from 2 to 21 in a state of gradual erosion, beginning with territorial expansion and ending with the loss of its core holdings. At the start, the Compact held Trostead and occupied Geirnpei Shafts, a site it had taken from no one in year 2. Its power was reinforced by three marriages to House Buldbei in years 2, 2, and 6, binding the two powers together. Faellwil Na ruled at the beginning, but his reign ended in year 4 when he was killed by Lald Neirntean at Trostead. The succession passed to Neinos Del, the named heir, whose claim was upheld against Frernli Peimeath. Neinos Del died in year 5, and Neallseis Staend took the seat by right of birth.

Neallseis Staend’s rule was short and violent. In year 10, Paepu Noth challenged him and won, taking the seat by coup and casting Neallseis out. Paepu Noth’s tenure was marked by the collapse of the Compact’s authority. Pellweagate rose against the Compact in year 10, breaking away as the Rising of Pellweagate in year 16 after the Compact’s standing had fallen to nothing. Geirnpei Shafts also rose in year 15 for the same reason. The Compact fought two wars against House Buldbei, losing both at Pei Delve in years 9 and 10. It sent four raids, all carrying off plunder, and suffered ten raids, four of which carried off plunder. The period saw eight years of hunger, killing 209 people and displacing 214.

Kesfound Thrael took the seat in year 17 after Paepu Noth was killed by Lald Neirntean. The Compact’s fortunes shifted briefly when it defeated the Galweall League at Geirnpei Shafts in year 19, but it lost that same site to the Galweall League in year 20. Kesfound Thrael was killed by Lald Neirntean at Themoor in year 20. Vun Doultheam took the seat by right of birth in year 20 and held it at the end of the period. The Compact had lost Pellweagate and Geirnpei Shafts, and its standing had fallen to nothing in the places that had risen against it. The period ended with the Compact exhausted and diminished, having fought four battles, winning one and losing three.

### House Buldbei, 3–22

House Buldbei’s rule from year 3 to 22 was defined by internal violence and a persistent war with the Trostead Compact. The period began with Skengaer Tharsoull holding the seat, but his tenure ended quickly when Trurnur Draeltheas had him murdered at Micrag in year 7. Threth Kruth took the seat next by the strongest claim, but Trurnur Draeltheas challenged and won, taking the seat by coup in year 8. Trurnur Draeltheas was then killed by Lall Nealdgum at Micrag in year 9. Kesfound Thrael took the seat by the strongest claim in year 9, but Lall Nealdgum challenged and won in year 12, taking the seat by coup. Lall Nealdgum held the seat until the end of the period. Five people held the seat in twenty years, two of them killed.

The house fought two battles against the Trostead Compact at Pei Delve, winning both. In year 9, House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Trostead Compact, killing 23. In year 10, they defeated the Trostead Compact again, killing 18. The war lasted two years before peace was made in year 11. House Buldbei sent four raids during this period. A raid on Trostead in year 4 was beaten off. Raids on Pellweagate in year 10 and Geirnpei Shafts in year 11 got through but took nothing. A raid on Trostead in year 20 carried off 32 grain and killed 26. The house suffered one raid, on Micrag in year 22, where the Trostead Compact carried off 28 grain and killed 48.

Internal stability was low. Two people were cast out as the losing side of a coup. One person was declared outlaw. Two people were murdered from within. Pei Delve rose against House Buldbei in year 9, when its standing had fallen to nothing. Hunger at Pei Delve killed 41 people over four years. Seven people were courted away from its ruler. The quarrel between House Buldbei and the Trostead Compact continued through the period, with two battles fought between them.

### The Rising of Pellweagate, 16–45

The Rising of Pellweagate spent thirty years as a fractured power, defined by its separation from the Trostead Compact and its eventual destruction by the Galweall League. It began in year 16 when Veith Hei took the seat after Pellweagate broke from the Compact. The period was marked by internal instability, with the seat passing between four rulers and five people cast out. External pressure came from repeated failed raids and a final war that ended the power.

Veith Hei held the seat until year 32, when Find Bound challenged him openly and won. Veith Hei was cast out. Find Bound’s rule ended in year 41 after an attempt on Lan Thraestun failed and was traced back; he was cast out for attempted murder. Neallseis Staend took the seat by election, setting aside the claim of the named heir Lurntu Skondbea. Neallseis died in year 42. Besveir Maleal took the seat by election after Neallseis’s death. His house ended when the power was finished.

The power suffered three famines in years 19, 23, and 27, killing 27, 55, and 120 people respectively. It sent five raids, all beaten off: on Trostead in years 19 and 35, on Gaehollow in 31, on Themoor in 32, and on Geirnpei Shafts in 33. It suffered one raid on Pellweagate in year 43, which killed 31 but took nothing. It ordered four killings against people of other powers: Skind Pesvi in 42, Lurntu Skondbea in 43, Dasould Ream in 44, and Veith Hei in 44.

In year 44, the Galweall League declared war over a long-standing grievance. The Rising of Pellweagate fought one battle, lost to the Galweall League at Pellweagate with 77 dead. The Galweall League took Pellweagate from the power. The Rising of Pellweagate was finished, landless, with its last 7 followers scattering. Peace was made in year 45.

### The Galweall League, 22–41

The Galweall League’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by internal instability and external aggression. At the start, the League held the seat through Threth Kruth, whose rule ended in 27 after an attempt on Del Monbirn failed. The period saw five people hold the seat, with the position passing four times. Threth Kruth was cast out, followed by Dreamwein Vous, who was cast out in 36 after a coup by Bathwa Bea. Bathwa Bea was cast out in 37 after a coup by Thaham Geth, who was cast out in 40 after losing an open challenge to Lan Thraestun. Lan Thraestun held the seat at the end of the period. The League suffered ten raids, all beaten off, and sent four raids, three of which carried off plunder. Two wars were begun, lasting one year in total.

Internal purges were frequent. Ten people were cast out: four for attempted murder, three for losing open challenges, two for losing coups, and one for conspiracy. Three people already gone were declared outlaws. Two exiles returned to service. The League also ordered one killing against outsiders, Tha Vu at Trostead in 30, and suffered two internal murders. Hunger struck in 23, killing 192 people across Themoor and Gaehollow. Marriages tied the League to the Rising of Pellweagate in 25 and 41.

Externally, the League defeated the Trostead Compact at Trostead in 29, taking the settlement and finishing the Compact. It defeated House Buldbei at Geirnpei Shafts in 41. Despite these victories, the League faced constant pressure, with 29 people courted away from its ruler. The period ended with Lan Thraestun holding the seat, the League having survived its internal fractures and external threats but remaining engaged in conflict.

### House Buldbei, 23–42

The period from 23 to 42 was defined by the collapse of House Buldbei’s authority and its subsequent military failure against the Galweall League. At the start, the house was already weakened by internal betrayal; Beasre Feanwoul attempted to murder Lall Nealdgum in 24 and was cast out, then declared an outlaw in 26 for a conspiracy uncovered after three years. The house’s standing fell to nothing by 28, when Pei Delve rose against it. This loss of control coincided with severe famine at Micrag in 26 and 27, which killed 275 people and drove 273 from their homes.

Lall Nealdgum held the seat until his death in 40. During his rule, House Buldbei sent four raids against the Galweall League, all of which were beaten off at Geirnpei Shafts in 30, Gaehollow in 31, Themoor in 32, and Trostead in 39. The house also suffered three raids from the Galweall League, which carried off plunder from Micrag in 31 and Pei Delve in 33, and took nothing from Micrag in 41. The conflict escalated in 41 when House Buldbei declared war on the Galweall League over a long-standing grievance, leading to two defeats at Geirnpei Shafts, with 12 dead in 41 and 10 dead in 42.

The seat passed to Dasould Ream in 40 after his rival Bathwa Bea’s claim was rejected and Bea was cast out. Dasould Ream remained in power at the end of the period. His rule was marked by violence within his own ranks, ordering the murder of Lan Thraestun at Themoor in 42. Two exiles, Bathwa Bea and Dasould Ream, had returned to service in 38. The period ended with House Buldbei still holding the seat but having lost two battles and seen its standing fall from nothing to a continued state of conflict.

### The Galweall League, 42–51

The Galweall League’s decade was defined by the fragility of its rule and the persistence of its enemies. Its standing fell to nothing in 42 after Haeldkeind Nen was cast out, prompting Geirnpei Shafts to rise against it. The League survived this internal collapse and external pressure, but its leadership cycled rapidly. Ten people held the seat in ten years, with the average tenure lasting less than a year. The period ended with Fear Mel still holding the seat after a coup in 51, having defeated House Buldbei in battle just days prior.

Lan Thraestun began the period holding the seat, but his rule ended when he killed Del Monbirn at Themoor in 42, only to be killed himself by Dasould Ream later that same year. Laelldou Nerum took the seat by setting aside the named heir Haeldkeind Nen in an election. Nerum’s rule was short; he was killed by Dasould Ream at Themoor in 43. Veith Hei then took the seat by setting aside the named heir Hibrei Fou. Hei’s rule ended when he was killed by Besveir Maleal at Themoor in 44.

Haeldkeind Nen returned to take the seat by election in 44, but lost it to Nond Mea in an open challenge in 45. Nond Mea’s rule was marked by internal conspiracy; Keandtei Taegro and Vaswei Grand were uncovered and cast out or executed for plots against him. Rusund Gilteil challenged Nond Mea and won, taking the seat by coup in 47 and killing Nond Mea at Themoor. Was Leithwon had Rusund Gilteil murdered at Themoor in 48. Varnnael Wansen then took the seat by setting aside the named heir Peansith Rae in an election.

Rend Greall had Varnnael Wansen murdered at Themoor in 50. Mound Sith took the seat by election in 50. Greimfourn Goul challenged Mound Sith openly in 51, won, and took the seat, killing Mound Sith at Themoor. Fear Mel then challenged Greimfourn Goul, won, and took the seat by coup in 51. Throughout the period, the League fought three battles, all won: against House Buldbei at Geirnpei Shafts in 42, against the Rising of Pellweagate at Pellweagate in 44, and against House Buldbei at Pei Delve in 51. It took two places: Pellweagate from the Rising of Pellweagate in 44 and Pei Delve from House Buldbei in 51. The League sent one raid, which got through but took nothing, and suffered two raids, both beaten off. Six of its own people were murdered from within, and seven were cast out.

### House Buldbei, 43–51

The period from 43 to 51 was defined by internal collapse and external defeat. House Buldbei began with a ruler who ordered a killing and ended with a new ruler who had lost a battle and a place. The house survived the years, but its authority was broken. Three people held the seat in nine years. Two were killed. One remained. The seat passed from one person to another two times inside this period.

Dasould Ream held the seat at the start. In 43, he had Laelldou Nerum murdered at Themoor. That same year, House Buldbei and the Galweall League made peace after two years of war. In 44, Dasould Ream was killed by Thaham Geth at Micrag. Brolgou Hald took the seat of House Buldbei because Dasould Ream was dead and no claimant remained. Brolgou Hald ruled until 50.

In 45, Find Bound returned from exile and took service with House Buldbei. That year, House Buldbei’s raid on Pellweagate was beaten off. In 46, Hibrei Fou returned from exile and took service with House Buldbei. In 47, Haeldkeind Nen returned from exile and took service with House Buldbei. In 48, Vaswei Grand returned from exile and took service with House Buldbei. Hibrei Fou won Find Bound away in 48. In 49, House Buldbei’s raid on Geirnpei Shafts was beaten off.

In 50, Hibrei Fou had Brolgou Hald murdered at Micrag. Brolgou Hald, ruler of House Buldbei, was killed by Hibrei Fou at Micrag. Find Bound took the seat of House Buldbei by the strongest claim because Brolgou Hald was dead. In 51, Was Leithwon returned from exile and took service with House Buldbei. The Galweall League declared war on House Buldbei over a long-standing grievance. The Galweall League defeated House Buldbei at Pei Delve. Twelve people died. The Galweall League took Pei Delve from House Buldbei. The period ended in 51.


## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Find Bound over House Buldbei, 50–51

Find Bound took the seat of House Buldbei in year 50, succeeding Brolgou Hald, who had been killed by Hibrei Fou at Micrag. The succession followed the strongest claim. In year 51, Drean Stulnir was born to Find Bound at Micrag. Was Leithwon returned from exile that same year and took service with the house.

The Galweall League declared war on House Buldbei in year 51 over a long-standing grievance. House Buldbei spent 54 silver buying goodwill for the third time, but was not believed. The Galweall League defeated House Buldbei at Pei Delve, where 12 people died, and took Pei Delve from them.

### The rule of Fear Mel over the Galweall League, 51–51

In 51, Greimfourn Goul challenged Mound Sith openly for the Galweall League and won the seat. Mound Sith was killed by Greimfourn Goul at Themoor later that year. Fear Mel challenged Greimfourn Goul for the Galweall League and won. Fear Mel took the seat of the Galweall League by coup. Greimfourn Goul was cast out of the Galweall League.

The Galweall League declared war on House Buldbei over a long-standing grievance. The Galweall League spent 140 silver to buy back goodwill. The Galweall League defeated House Buldbei at Pei Delve, with 12 dead. The Galweall League took Pei Delve from House Buldbei.

Beasre Feanwoul won Besveir Maleal away from the ruler of the Galweall League. Findgeild De married Hawol Leam at Themoor. Nounwern Beth was born to Rend Greall at Themoor. The period ended in 51.
