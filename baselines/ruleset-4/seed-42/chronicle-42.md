Seed 42, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War for Threi Cut: the Griwick Compact against the Wurn League, 5–10

The Griwick Compact declared war on the Wurn League in year 5 for Threi Cut. The Wurn League defeated the Griwick Compact at Threi Cut in year 5, with 45 dead. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Threi Cut in year 6, with 35 dead. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Threi Cut in year 7, with 29 dead. The Griwick Compact took Threi Cut from the Wurn League in year 7.

The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Laehiford in year 8, with 107 dead. The Griwick Compact took Laehiford from the Wurn League in year 8. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Hadale in year 9, with 101 dead. The Griwick Compact and the Wurn League made peace after 5 years in year 10, both sides exhausted.

### The War for Griwick: the Kebarrow Compact against the Griwick Compact, 27–29

The war began in 27 when the Kebarrow Compact declared it on the Griwick Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Griwick Compact defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Vea Lode in the same year, with 57 dead. The Griwick Compact defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Vea Lode again in 28, with 48 dead.

In 28, Sou Dra had Thold Valmaer murdered at Griwick. The conflict continued until 29, when the Kebarrow Compact and the Griwick Compact made peace after two years, both sides exhausted.

### The Kebarrow Compact, 2–21

The Kebarrow Compact’s twenty-year span from 2 to 21 was defined by internal instability and a cycle of retaliatory violence. The period began with a marriage binding the Compact to the Griwick Compact, but ended with Sou Dra still holding the seat after a failed challenge. Five people held the seat during this time, with the position passing four times. The Compact’s standing deteriorated significantly, marked by a rebellion in 15 that followed the outlawing of Witrou Breim.

Searn Sisrill ruled until 8, when Math Ham killed him at Kebarrow. Reweld Wul took the seat by election and served for six years. His rule ended when Witrou Breim had him murdered at Kebarrow in 14. Witrou Breim then took the seat by election. He faced an open challenge from Pouldrir Ho in 15, losing the seat and being cast out. Pouldrir Ho ruled for five years until Turaer Danpa had him murdered at Kebarrow in 20. Sou Dra took the seat by election in 20 and held it through 21, surviving an open challenge from Thra Bround that year.

The Compact engaged in eight raids, three of which carried off plunder, two got through but took nothing, and three were beaten off. It suffered six raids, all by the Griwick Compact or the Wurn League. Four of its own people were murdered from within. Two were cast out, and one was declared outlaw. Four exiles returned to serve the Compact. Hunger at Meigate in 10 killed 23 people. Meigate rose against the Compact in 15, when its standing had fallen very low. Four people were courted away from its ruler.

### The Kebarrow Compact, 22–40

The Kebarrow Compact’s nineteen-year tenure from 22 to 40 was defined by a relentless war with the Griwick Compact and a succession of internal purges that left its leadership fractured. The period began with Sou Dra holding the seat, a position he maintained through open challenges and the execution of conspirators, but ended with the Compact finished, its land lost and its followers scattered. Five people held the seat during this time, each ruling for an average of 3.4 years, with the seat passing four times. The Compact fought four battles, all lost to the Griwick Compact, and suffered two major defeats at Kebarrow that directly precipitated its destruction.

Sou Dra’s rule was marked by violent consolidation and external aggression. He survived a conspiracy by Turaer Danpa in 25, leading to Danpa’s execution, and later ordered the murder of Thold Valmaer at Griwick in 28. His authority was challenged openly by Tor Nathgoull in 29; Sou Dra lost the seat and was cast out. Tor Nathgoull then took the seat by coup after winning a challenge in 32, but his rule was short-lived. He was cast out in 35 after losing an open challenge to Beas Krouthea. Beas Krouthea’s tenure ended in 38 when he was murdered by Kou Peis at Kebarrow, an act of internal violence that left the seat vacant until Diweith Mound took it by election later that year.

The external conflict with the Griwick Compact escalated throughout the period. The Kebarrow Compact sent four raids, two of which were beaten off, one that took nothing, and one that carried off plunder. In retaliation, the Griwick Compact raided Kebarrow in 26, carrying off grain. The two powers fought four battles, all lost by the Kebarrow Compact: at Vea Lode in 27 and 28, and at Kebarrow in 38 and 39. The defeat at Kebarrow in 39 resulted in the Griwick Compact taking the settlement from the Kebarrow Compact. In the same year, the Kebarrow Compact was finished, its last nine followers scattering. The period ended with the Griwick Compact making peace after the collapse of its rival.

### The Wurn League, 22–35

The Wurn League’s fourteen-year span from 22 to 35 was defined by internal fragmentation and external defeat. The period began with Trem Lolkoll holding the seat, but his rule was immediately undermined by conspiracy. Befu Seirn’s plot against him was uncovered in 22, leading to Seirn’s execution. This instability marked the start of a rapid succession of rulers, with the seat passing eight times among nine individuals. The League’s authority eroded through a series of violent takeovers and purges, culminating in its destruction by the Griwick Compact.

Internal strife drove the early years. Nael War’s conspiracy against Trem Lolkoll was uncovered in 25, resulting in War’s exile. In 26, Gatros Hearn challenged and defeated Trem Lolkoll, taking the seat by coup. Sothkel Sald then challenged Hearn, winning and taking the seat by coup, before killing Hearn at Hadale. Sald himself was challenged by Saern Meastouth, who took the seat by coup and killed Sald at Hadale. Saern’s rule ended in 27 when his attempt on Thold Valmaer failed, leading to his exile. Teapeall Heampim took the seat by right of birth, ruling until his death in 28. Paernrom Sir contested the claim of the named heir Bu Rumpirn, winning by primogeniture and taking the seat, while Bu Rumpirn was cast out. Paernrom Sir was murdered by Stonand Ker in 30, and Heillvar Maer took the seat by right of birth. Heillvar’s rule ended in 32 after his attempt on Nael War failed, leading to his exile. Drarka Draernthun took the seat by right of birth.

The League’s external conflicts mirrored its internal decay. It suffered one raid by the Griwick Compact in 24, which carried off plunder from Hadale. Three raids sent by the League against the Griwick Compact in 29 and 31 were all beaten off. In 33, the Griwick Compact declared war on the Wurn League. Stonand Ker challenged and defeated Drarka Draernthun, taking the seat by coup. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Hadale in 33, killing 58, and again in 34, killing 87. In 34, the Griwick Compact took Hadale from the Wurn League. The Wurn League was finished, its last 25 followers scattering. Peace was made between the Griwick Compact and the Wurn League in 35, after the League’s collapse.

### The Meigate Covenant, 29–51

The Meigate Covenant began in 29 as a breakaway from the Kebarrow Compact, with Raes Go taking the seat. Its early years were defined by failed aggression and internal consolidation. Raes Go married Mothgall Vafarn in 30, binding the Covenant to the Vea Lode Covenant. Raids on Griwick in 31 and Laehiford in 33 were both beaten off. The ruler’s position was challenged openly in 43 by Heillvar Maer, who lost and was cast out. Raes Go himself was cast out in 44 after losing an open challenge to Diweith Mound. Diweith Mound ruled until he was killed in 46, at which point Drarka Draernthun took the seat by setting aside the claim of the named heir Tor Nathgoull.

The period saw two wars with the Griwick Compact, one of which lasted two years. The Covenant declared war in 47 over a long-standing grievance. It raided Griwick in 47, carrying off 23 grain and killing 16, but was defeated at Threi Cut later that year with 12 dead. It was defeated again at Threi Cut in 48 with 10 dead. Peace was made in 49. The Covenant suffered raids from the Griwick Compact in 42 and 45. It sent eight raids in total; four were beaten off, and four carried off plunder. Three marriages tied the Covenant to other powers: the Vea Lode Covenant in 30, and the Griwick Compact in 35 and 50.

The Covenant’s standing fell to nothing in 45 when Vea Lode rose against it. Hunger killed 68 people across the period, with 61 dying at Meigate in 38. Eleven exiles returned to service. Three people were courted away from its ruler. The period ended in 51 with Drarka Draernthun still holding the seat. The seat passed from one person to another two times inside this period. Two battles were fought, both lost. One war was begun. One place was taken.

### The rule of Paernmel Has over the Griwick Compact, 50–51

In year 50, Paernmel Has challenged Raes Go openly for the Griwick Compact and won the seat. He killed Raes Go at Griwick later that same year. Tor Nathgoull was raised to steward of the Griwick Compact in year 50.

In year 51, Visal Veill married Treild Haen at Hadale. Paernmel Has’s child, Gim Raeldrol, was born at Kebarrow. Voudreirn Wer, Trurkeand Busi, and Ta Poveil each won Bakrei Dea away from the ruler of the Griwick Compact. The Griwick Compact spent 205 silver buying goodwill for the fifth time and was not believed. The Meigate Covenant raided Hadale, carrying off 49 grain and killing 14.
