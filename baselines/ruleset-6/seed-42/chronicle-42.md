### The War for Threi Cut: the Griwick Compact against the Wurn League, 5–10

The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Laehiford in year 8, with 107 dead. The Griwick Compact took Laehiford from the Wurn League in year 8. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Hadale in year 9, with 101 dead. The Griwick Compact and the Wurn League made peace in year 10 after 5 years, both sides exhausted.

### The War for Griwick: the Kebarrow Compact against the Griwick Compact, 27–29

The war began in 27 when the Kebarrow Compact declared it on the Griwick Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Griwick Compact defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Vea Lode in the same year, with 57 dead. The Griwick Compact defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Vea Lode again in 28, with 48 dead.

In 28, Sou Dra had Thold Valmaer murdered at Griwick. The Kebarrow Compact and the Griwick Compact made peace in 29 after two years, both sides exhausted.

### The Griwick Compact, 4–23

The Griwick Compact’s twenty-year span from 4 to 23 was defined by a war with the Wurn League that began in 5 and ended in 18, five years of fighting that consumed the power’s early energy. The conflict started when the Compact declared war for Threi Cut, broke its alliance with the Wurn League, and lost a battle at Threi Cut in 5 with 45 dead. It reversed this defeat by winning three successive battles against the Wurn League at Threi Cut in 6 and 7, and at Laehiford in 8, taking Threi Cut and Laehiford from the League. The war ended in 18 with both sides exhausted. During this period, the Compact sent six raids, all carrying off plunder, and suffered ten raids, six of which were beaten off.

Internal stability fractured under the rule of Kei Leassel, who took the seat in 9 by setting aside the claim of the named heir Turaer Danpa. Kei Leassel had Math Ham murdered at Hadale in 10. His rule ended when he died in 17. Before his death, the Compact’s standing had fallen very low, prompting Vea Lode to rise against it in 9, and fallen to nothing, prompting Threi Cut to rise against it in 12. Three people were cast out during this period: Pouldrir Ho for attempted murder in 9, Turaer Danpa for the losing claim in 9, and Beirn Naem for the losing claim in 17. One exile, Witrou Breim, returned in 17.

Witrou Breim took the seat in 17 after rejecting the rival claim of Beirn Naem. He held the seat until he was killed by Bu Rumpirn at Griwick in 23. Thold Valmaer took the seat in 23 by the strongest claim. The period ended with Thold Valmaer still holding the seat. Four people held the seat in these twenty years, with an average tenure of 4.7 years. The Griwick Compact finished the period with its standing diminished by internal purges and external uprisings, though it retained its territorial gains from the Wurn League.

### The Wurn League, 22–35

The Wurn League’s fourteen-year span from 22 to 35 was defined by internal fragmentation and external defeat. At the start, the League was bound to the Kebarrow Compact through marriages in 23, 25, and 28, yet its leadership cycled rapidly. Nine people held the seat, passing eight times, with an average tenure of 1.3 years. The period ended with the League finished, its land lost and its followers scattered, following a war with the Griwick Compact that it lost in two battles.

Trem Lolkoll held the seat at the beginning, but his rule was undermined by conspiracies. Befu Seirn’s plot was uncovered in 22, leading to Seirn’s execution. Nael War’s conspiracy was uncovered in 25, resulting in War’s exile. In 26, Gatros Hearn challenged Trem Lolkoll openly, won, and took the seat. Sothkel Sald then challenged Hearn, won, and took the seat by coup, killing Hearn at Hadale. Saern Meastouth challenged Sald, won, and took the seat by coup, killing Sald at Hadale. Meastouth’s rule was short; his attempt on Thold Valmaer failed in 27, and he was cast out. Teapeall Heampim took the seat by right of birth and died in 28. Paernrom Sir contested the claim of the named heir Bu Rumpirn, won by primogeniture, and took the seat, casting out Rumpirn.

The League’s military and diplomatic position deteriorated. It suffered one raid on Hadale in 24 by the Griwick Compact, which carried off 34 grain. Its own raids on Vea Lode in 29, Laehiford in 29, and Threi Cut in 31 were all beaten off. In 30, Stonand Ker had Paernrom Sir murdered at Hadale. Heillvar Maer took the seat by right of birth. In 31, Teillmol Lund’s conspiracy was uncovered, and he was executed. Heillvar Maer’s attempt on Nael War failed in 32, and he was cast out. Drarka Draernthun took the seat by right of birth.

In 33, the Griwick Compact declared war on the Wurn League over a long-standing grievance and broke its alliance with the League. Stonand Ker challenged Drarka Draernthun, won, and took the seat by coup, casting out Draernthun. Thosruld Lul’s conspiracy was uncovered, and he was executed. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Hadale in 33, with 58 dead. In 34, Leimmil Theall’s attempt on Stonand Ker failed, and he was cast out. Stonand Ker had Nael War murdered at Griwick. Theall’s conspiracy was uncovered, and he was declared outlaw. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Hadale in 34, with 87 dead, and took Hadale from the League. The Wurn League was finished, its last 25 followers scattering. Peace was made between the Griwick Compact and the Wurn League in 35.

### The Vea Lode Covenant, 29–39

The Vea Lode Covenant began in year 29 when Vea Lode broke from the Griwick Compact, with Mothgall Vafarn taking the seat. The new power faced immediate hardship, as famine struck Vea Lode in year 30, leaving grain short and killing 11 people. This disaster initiated a prolonged decline, with the famine continuing for three more years. In year 31, 13 died and 32 abandoned the place. In year 32, 9 died and 22 left. In year 33, 7 died and 30 abandoned the place. The internal stability of the Covenant also fractured during this time. In year 33, Sou Dra had Mothgall Vafarn murdered at Vea Lode. Sou Dra then took the seat by election.

The Covenant shifted from internal survival to external aggression. In year 34, it raided Griwick, carrying off 36 grain and killing 20. In year 35, it raided Laehiford, carrying off 44 grain and killing 28. These raids were followed by another internal purge. In year 36, Bu Rumpirn had Sou Dra murdered at Vea Lode. Bu Rumpirn took the seat by election. He held the position until his death in year 39 at the age of 60.

The period ended with the dissolution of the power. In year 39, the Vea Lode Covenant came to an end. Its single place fell to no one. The succession by election had produced three rulers: Mothgall Vafarn, Sou Dra, and Bu Rumpirn. The initial break from the Griwick Compact did not secure the Covenant’s longevity, as it ended without a successor holding the seat.

### The Griwick Compact, 44–51

The Griwick Compact endured eight years of violent internal turnover and a single, protracted war with the Meigate Covenant. The period began with the murder of Throll Kell by Lorn Waldhith at Griwick in 44, an act that allowed Tum Ga to seize the seat by the strongest claim. Tum Ga’s rule was brief; he was challenged and defeated by Stonand Ker in 45, who took the seat by coup and cast Tum Ga out. The Compact’s stability remained fragile as Lorn Waldhith’s conspiracy against Stonand Ker was uncovered in 45, leading to Lorn’s exile. Stonand Ker’s tenure ended in 47 when Kou Peis murdered him at Hadale. Kou Peis’s own rule was short-lived, ending in 48 when his conspiracy against the new ruler, Thres Thrild, was uncovered and he was executed. Thres Thrild held the seat until 49, when Raes Go challenged and defeated him, taking the seat by coup and casting Thres out. Raes Go’s rule ended in 50 when Paernmel Has openly challenged and defeated him, killing Raes at Griwick and taking the seat. Paernmel Has held the seat as the period closed in 51.

The Compact’s external relations were defined by its conflict with the Meigate Covenant. In 45, the Compact raided Meigate, killing 29 but taking nothing. In 47, the Meigate Covenant declared war and broke its alliance with the Compact. The Meigate Covenant raided Griwick in 47, carrying off 23 grain and killing 16. The Compact defeated the Meigate Covenant at Threi Cut later that year, with 12 dead. The Compact defeated the Meigate Covenant at Threi Cut again in 48, with 10 dead. The war lasted two years, ending in 49 when both sides made peace, exhausted. The Meigate Covenant raided Laehiford in 49, but the raid was beaten off. In 51, the Meigate Covenant raided Hadale, carrying off 49 grain and killing 14.

Internal cohesion eroded throughout the period. Four people were cast out: Tum Ga and Thres Thrild as the losing side of coups, Lorn Waldhith for conspiracy, and Drouldthas Stour for a losing claim. Three exiles returned to service: Raes Go in 45, Heillvar Maer in 46, and Tor Nathgoull in 49. The Compact suffered four killings from within its own people: Throll Kell, Stonand Ker, Kou Peis, and Raes Go. The Compact ordered one killing against a person of another power: Diweith Mound at Meigate in 46. Six people held the seat in eight years, with an average tenure of 1.1 years. The seat passed five times. The Compact fought two battles, both won. One war was begun.
