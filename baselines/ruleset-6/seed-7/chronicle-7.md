# A history of the Rulllound Vale

Seed 7, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Goummeidale Compact's Grudge: the Goummeidale Compact against House Thream, 5–7

In year 5, the Goummeidale Compact declared war on House Thream over the site of Sti Seam. The conflict began with a decisive engagement at that location, where the Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream, leaving 34 dead. The fighting continued into year 6, when the Goummeidale Compact again defeated House Thream at Sti Seam, resulting in 28 deaths. Following this second victory, the Goummeidale Compact took Sti Seam from House Thream.

The war lasted for two years. In year 7, the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream made peace, with both sides exhausted.

### The Long Quarrel over Kraeford: the Goummeidale Compact against the Kraeford Compact, 21–23

The Goummeidale Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact in 21, citing a long-standing grievance. In that same year, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, where 110 people died, and took the settlement from the Kraeford Compact.

In 22, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, where 181 people died, and took the settlement from the Kraeford Compact.

In 23, the Goummeidale Compact and the Kraeford Compact made peace after two years, following the collapse of the Kraeford Compact.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### House Thream, 2–29

House Thream began the period as a power bound by alliance to the Kraeford Compact, but its standing was defined by a succession of six rulers who held the seat for an average of 4.3 years each. The house’s internal stability was fragile, with the seat passing five times through death, killing, and contested claims. Its external relations were dominated by a quarrel with the Goummeidale Compact that escalated from a broken alliance into a war of attrition, resulting in the loss of two key settlements and the eventual destruction of the house itself.

The period opened with the death of Freamteas Kraeth in year 3, after which Bran Nas took the seat by the strongest claim. Early in the reign, a famine at Sti Seam in year 4 killed six people and left grain short by two. This shortage triggered a war in year 5 when the Goummeidale Compact declared war on House Thream for Sti Seam and broke its alliance with the house. House Thream raided Seithfaehollow, carrying off 27 grain and killing 35, but was defeated at Sti Seam with 34 dead. The Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream again at Sti Seam in year 6, taking the settlement. In the same year, Kokaes Threll took the seat by setting aside the claim of the named heir Ma Graltaeld, who was cast out. Vinbeil Real returned from exile and took service with the house in year 7, and peace was made with the Goummeidale Compact after two years of war.

Kokaes Threll was murdered by Fous Bo in year 8, and Vinbeil Real took the seat by the strongest claim. Fous Bo’s conspiracy against Vinbeil was uncovered in year 10, and Fous Bo was cast out. House Thream’s raids on Goummeidale in year 16, Kreagemoor in year 18, Sti Seam in year 19, Ra Cut in year 20, Seithfaehollow in year 22, and Kraeford in year 23 were all beaten off. A raid by the Goummeidale Compact on Loufell in year 24 killed 18 but took nothing. Vinbeil Real died in year 17, and Sen Tom took the seat in year 17. Maethhol Gondrur challenged Sen Tom openly in year 26, won, and took the seat, killing Sen Tom. The Goummeidale Compact declared war again in year 27 over a long-standing grievance, defeating House Thream at Loufell with 123 dead in year 27 and 103 dead in year 28, then taking Loufell. House Thream was finished in year 28, its landless followers scattering. The period ended in year 29 with peace between the Goummeidale Compact and the destroyed House Thream.

### The Kraeford Compact, 2–23

The Kraeford Compact’s twenty-two years ended in its destruction, a trajectory defined by internal instability and a fatal war with the Goummeidale Compact. The period began with an alliance between the Compact and House Thream, but the Compact’s leadership was immediately shaken by the murder of its ruler, Skur Stas, by Kraedren Waeld. Waeld’s subsequent challenge to the succession was rejected in an election that installed Stearnke Gullleith, who ruled until his death. The Compact’s standing eroded through failed raids, internal conspiracies, and famine, culminating in a war it lost entirely, resulting in the loss of its lands and the scattering of its followers.

Stearnke Gullleith’s rule was marked by a persistent threat from Kraedren Waeld, who had been cast out after his claim was rejected. Waeld’s conspiracy against Gullleith was uncovered after two years, leading to Waeld’s declaration as an outlaw. Gullleith died in 9, and Frouldtoun Dror took the seat by election. Dror’s tenure saw the Compact’s military efforts falter; a raid on Kreagemoor was beaten off, while a raid on Goummeidale successfully carried off plunder. However, the Compact suffered raids from House Thream and the Goummeidale Compact, and faced famines at Seithfaehollow and Kraeford that killed 74 people. Thral Rei returned from exile to serve the Compact, only to be executed for conspiracy against Dror.

The Compact’s end was sealed by its war with the Goummeidale Compact, which broke its alliance and declared war over a long-standing grievance. The Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow and Kraeford, taking both places. During this conflict, Dror had Kaell Frouspim murdered at Goummeidale. In 22, Theirnpae Gro married Wold Risla, binding the Compact to House Thream, but this alliance could not prevent the Compact’s defeat at Kraeford. The Compact was finished, its last two followers scattering, and peace was made with the Goummeidale Compact in 23.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 22–41

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by violent succession and the elimination of external rivals. The period began with the murder of Freindmald Beindlea by Waeld Dould at Goummeidale, an act that allowed Frouldtoun Dror to take the seat by right of birth. The Compact immediately secured its position by defeating the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, taking the settlement and ending that power. It then turned against House Thream, defeating them twice at Loufell in 27 and 28, taking Loufell and finishing House Thream. The Compact’s internal stability was fragile, marked by six murders committed by its own people and five castings out.

Frouldtoun Dror’s rule ended in 28 when he was cast out for an attempted murder. Voumfaern Ki took the seat but was soon defeated in an open challenge by Skullhein Threill. Skullhein was murdered by Vasgou Raend, who then seized the seat in a coup. Vasgou died in 34, and Wold Risla succeeded him by right of birth. Wold was cast out after losing an open challenge to Brethneld Draer. Brethneld was murdered by Seill Kreannae in 40, allowing Maethhol Gondrur to take the seat by right of birth. Maethhol held the seat at the end of the period.

The Compact suffered three raids, all beaten off, and sent one raid on Loufell that took nothing. It fought three battles, all won. Six people were courted away from its ruler. A plague at Seithfaehollow killed 106 people. The quarrel with House Thream resulted in two battles. The seat passed nine times. Ten people held the seat. Four were cast out, four were killed, one died, and one remained.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 42–50

The Goummeidale Compact’s rule in these years was defined by the survival of Maethhol Gondrur against a series of internal conspiracies that eroded its authority. At the start, the power faced a plague at Seithfaehollow that killed 103 people, followed by a second wave that claimed 150 lives and drove 171 others to flee. The seat was held by Maethhol Gondrur, who remained in power despite Seill Kreannae’s conspiracy being uncovered after four years. Kreannae was cast out of the Goummeidale Compact, but the threat to the seat persisted.

Thaeld Faeth’s conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered after three years, leading to Thaeld’s execution. Later, Gu Veam’s attempt on Maethhol’s life failed and was traced back, resulting in Gu’s exile from the Goummeidale Compact. Gu was later declared an outlaw for a new conspiracy uncovered after four years. Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy against Maethhol was also uncovered after three years, and Skein was cast out of the Goummeidale Compact.

The period ended with the rise of Sti Seam against the Goummeidale Compact, which had fallen to nothing in standing. Maethhol Gondrur remained the holder of the seat until the end of the period, but the power itself was destroyed by the uprising. The Goummeidale Compact ceased to exist as a ruling power, with Sti Seam taking control.
