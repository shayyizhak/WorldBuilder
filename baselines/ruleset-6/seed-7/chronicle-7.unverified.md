## The Goummeidale Compact, 2–21

- `ambiguous-short-name` — two powers here are called "compact"; the short form says neither
- `incomplete-enumeration` — …Three exiles returned to take service with the Compact: Ma Graltaeld in year 7, Kraedren …… — the sentence says 3 and names 2
- `out-of-order` — the killing of Sealoll Lael in 12 is what opened the seat, and is told after Fous Bo takes it

The Goummeidale Compact began the period holding Ra Cut, a site no one else held, and ended it with Seithfaehollow, having taken it from the Kraeford Compact. Four people held the seat over the twenty years, each ruling for an average of 4.7 years. The power survived three internal purges and two external wars, though its standing fell so low that two of its own places rose against it. The period closed with Freindmald Beindlea still holding the seat, having just taken it after the death of his predecessor.

Sealoll Lael held the seat at the start, binding the Compact to the Kraeford Compact through the marriage of Kaell Frouspim and Frouldtoun Dror in year 3, and to House Thream through the marriage of Freindmald Beindlea and Fous Bo in year 5. Sealoll Lael’s rule ended in year 12 when Thral Rei had him murdered at Goummeidale. Fous Bo took the seat by right of birth. His rule was marked by the murder of Sealoll Lael and a subsequent raid by the Kraeford Compact on Goummeidale that carried off 34 grain and killed 55. Fous Bo’s own rule ended in year 19 when Kraedren Waeld had him murdered at Goummeidale.

Kaell Frouspim took the seat by right of birth in year 19. His rule was short, ending in year 21 when Frouldtoun Dror had him killed at Goummeidale. Freindmald Beindlea took the seat by right of birth in year 21. During the period, the Goummeidale Compact fought three battles, winning all of them: it defeated House Thream at Sti Seam in year 5 with 34 dead, defeated House Thream at Sti Seam in year 6 with 28 dead, and defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow in year 21 with 110 dead. It took three places: Ra Cut in year 2, Sti Seam from House Thream in year 6, and Seithfaehollow from the Kraeford Compact in year 21.

The Compact sent out two raids, both carrying off plunder: one against Kraeford in year 13 and one against Seithfaehollow in year 16. It suffered eight raids, all beaten off except one by the Kraeford Compact on Goummeidale in year 12. Three people were cast out for conspiracy against the seat: Vinbeil Real in year 5, Thral Rei in year 13, and Kraedren Waeld in year 20. Three exiles returned to take service with the Compact: Ma Graltaeld in year 7, Kraedren Waeld in year 9, and Fous Bo in year 11. Two places rose against the Compact because its standing had fallen very low: Ra Cut in year 13 and Sti Seam in year 18.

Hunger and sickness lasted for eleven years, killing 364 people. Hunger struck Ra Cut in years 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 11, and Sti Seam in years 8, 9, and 15. Sickness struck Kreagemoor in years 14, 15, 16, and 17. The quarrel between the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream persisted, resulting in two battles between them. The Compact declared war on House Thream in year 5 and on the Kraeford Compact in year 21, spending two years at war in total.


## The rule of Maethhol Gondrur over the Goummeidale Compact, 40–51

- `wrong-killer` — …Maethhol Gondrur assumed the seat of the Goummeidale Compact in 40 by right of birth, fol…… — gondrur did not kill draer; kreannae did

Maethhol Gondrur assumed the seat of the Goummeidale Compact in 40 by right of birth, following the killing of Brethneld Draer by Seill Kreannae. The period began with a plague at Seithfaehollow that took 106 lives, a pestilence that burned out after one year before breaking out again in 42. That second outbreak claimed 103 people and lasted two years, during which 150 more died and 171 fled. Seill Kreannae’s conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered after four years, leading to Kreannae’s exile from the Goummeidale Compact.

Maethhol Gondrur’s rule faced internal dissent as Thaeld Faeth won Fol Skeambol away from the ruler in 44. Thaeld Faeth’s own conspiracy was uncovered after three years, resulting in his execution in 46. The Goummeidale Compact spent 185 silver to buy back goodwill that same year. Gu Veam’s attempt on Maethhol Gondrur failed in 47, and Gu Veam was cast out for attempted murder. Skein Teththeald won Meind Veild away from the ruler in 50, and Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy was uncovered after three years, leading to his exile.

The Goummeidale Compact’s standing had fallen to nothing by 50, when Sti Seam rose against it. Maethhol Gondrur’s rule ended in 51, the final year of the period. During this time, Maethhol Gondrur had children born at Loufell, including Hidol Krund, Faethhund Fam, Pan Dros, and Fraethrul Fri. The Goummeidale Compact finished with Sti Seam in open rebellion against it.
