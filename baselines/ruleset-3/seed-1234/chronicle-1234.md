# A history of the Rendfeall Vale

Seed 1234, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Trostead Compact's Grudge: the Trostead Compact against House Buldbei, 4–6

In year 4, the Trostead Compact declared war on House Buldbei over Pei Delve. The Compact defeated House Buldbei at Pei Delve in the same year, with 38 dead. In year 5, the Compact defeated House Buldbei again at Pei Delve, with 31 dead, and took Pei Delve from House Buldbei.

The war ended in year 6 after 2 years, with both sides exhausted.

### The the Trostead Compact Aggression: the Trostead Compact against the Galweall League, 7–10

The Trostead Compact declared war on the Galweall League in year 7, citing a long-standing grievance. The conflict began immediately with a defeat for the Compact at Gaehollow, where 90 of its forces died. In year 8, the Compact suffered another loss at Themoor, with 128 dead. During this same year, Neallseis Staend’s attempt on Lald Neirntean failed and was traced back.

The war continued into year 9 with a further defeat for the Compact at Gaehollow, resulting in 88 deaths. That year, Paepu Noth had Lald Neirntean murdered at Themoor. The fighting persisted until year 10, when the Trostead Compact and the Galweall League made peace after three years of conflict, both sides exhausted.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### The Galweall League, 2–21

The Galweall League spent twenty years defined by a binding alliance with House Buldbei and a violent, three-year war against the Trostead Compact. The period began with the League’s power consolidated through two marriages in year 2 that tied it to House Buldbei. Lald Neirntean held the seat from the start, but his rule ended in year 9 when he was killed by Paepu Noth at Themoor. The League’s standing was shaped by its ability to project force outward while absorbing blows from its rival. It sent seven raids against the Trostead Compact and other targets; four carried off plunder, while three were beaten off. It suffered six raids, most of which succeeded in taking grain and lives.

The conflict with the Trostead Compact dominated the middle of the period. The Compact declared war in year 7 over a long-standing grievance. The League and its allies defeated the Compact in three battles: at Gaehollow in year 7 with 90 dead, at Themoor in year 8 with 128 dead, and at Gaehollow in year 9 with 88 dead. These victories were followed by peace in year 10, leaving both sides exhausted. During the war, Lald Neirntean had Faellwil Na murdered at Trostead in year 4, and later Frernli Peimeath had Paepu Noth murdered at Trostead in year 17. The League also ordered the murder of Faellwil Na at Trostead in year 4.

Leadership changed hands three times. Gall Brolllea took the seat by election in year 9 after Lald’s death and died in year 10. Frernli Peimeath took the seat by election in year 10 and held it until his death in year 21. Threth Kruth challenged Frernli openly in year 18, lost, and was cast out. Five exiles returned to serve the League during this time. Sourmea Fruth took the seat by election in year 21, still holding it at the end of the period. The League finished the period with its alliance to House Buldbei intact, having survived the war and internal challenges.

### House Buldbei, 3–36

House Buldbei’s thirty-four years ended in ruin, its power extinguished by the Trostead Compact after a cycle of internal decay and external defeat. The period began with a marriage binding the house to the Compact, but it was defined by a succession crisis that fractured its leadership and a war it could not win. Three people held the seat, but only one died in office; the others were removed by violence or saw their house destroyed. The house fought three battles, all lost to the Trostead Compact, and lost two key places, Pei Delve and Micrag, to that same enemy.

The seat passed twice in this period. Skengaer Tharsoull held the seat since before year 3, but was killed by Lall Nealdgum at Micrag in year 9. Kesfound Thrael took the seat by the strongest claim in year 9, but was killed by Lall Nealdgum in year 11 after losing a challenge. Lall Nealdgum took the seat by coup in year 11. His rule was marked by conspiracy; Sealfeis Vearn was executed for conspiracy against him in year 34. Two other conspiracies, by Trurnur Draeltheas in year 6 and Threth Kruth in year 7, resulted in their castings out. Eight people were courted away from the ruler during this time, a pattern of internal erosion.

Famine struck four times, killing 255 people. Pei Delve suffered 14 deaths in year 3. Micrag suffered 154 in year 27, 35 in year 30, and 52 in year 32. The house sent eleven raids. Nine were beaten off. One raid on Pellweagate in year 14 got through but took nothing. One raid on Gaehollow in year 19 carried off 53 grain and killed 45. The Trostead Compact declared war in year 4 and year 35. In year 4, Buldbei lost 38 dead at Pei Delve and lost the place in year 5. In year 35, Buldbei lost 121 dead at Micrag and lost the place. The house was finished in year 35, its last 7 followers scattering.

### The Galweall League, 22–34

The Galweall League’s thirteen-year span from 22 to 34 was defined by internal purges and a final, fatal war with the Trostead Compact. The period began with Sourmea Fruth holding the seat, a position he retained through violence and challenge until 30. His rule was marked by the murder of Threth Kruth at Trostead in 23 and the subsequent murder of Find Bound at Themoor in 29. Fruth’s authority eroded as he lost followers to courtiers and faced multiple attempts on his life. In 30, Bathwa Bea challenged him openly, won, and took the seat, casting Fruth out.

Bathwa Bea’s rule was brief, ending in 31 when Neallseis Staend challenged him, won, and killed him at Themoor. Neallseis then held the seat until his own death at Gaehollow in 33. Throughout this time, the League suffered significant territorial losses, with Gaehollow breaking away to form the Gaehollow Charter in 30 and Themoor being taken by the Trostead Compact in 33. The League also endured a famine at Gaehollow in 30 that killed 51 people. Its military record was poor, with two raids sent out—one carrying off plunder and one beaten off—and one raid suffered from the Trostead Compact.

The conflict with the Trostead Compact escalated into war in 32. The Compact defeated the League at Themoor in 32, killing 104, and again in 33, killing 86. In 33, the Trostead Compact took Themoor from the League, and the Galweall League was finished, its last 14 followers scattering. The period ended in 34 with peace between the Trostead Compact and the remnants of the League. Three people held the seat during this time, with two killed and one cast out. The seat passed from one person to another two times inside this period.

### The Gaehollow Charter, 30–41

The Gaehollow Charter existed for twelve years, from 30 to 41, as a breakaway power that began with territorial separation and ended with total destruction. It stood at the start as a new entity, having severed ties with the Galweall League in 30 when Ran Skou took its seat. The period was defined by its isolation and eventual collapse, marked by a single war with the Trostead Compact that lasted one year and resulted in the loss of its only place, Gaehollow, and the scattering of its final three followers.

Ran Skou held the seat for nine years, during which the Charter expanded its reach through violence and diplomacy. In 31, it raided Trostead, carrying off 32 grain and killing 21. It swore an alliance with House Buldbei in 34. However, its military fortunes reversed in the late 30s, with raids on Geirnpei Shafts in 37 and Pei Delve in 38 both beaten off. The rule of Ran Skou ended in 39 when he was killed by Teamfeill Baeldful at Gaehollow.

Dealse Loundkuld took the seat in 39 after Ran Skou’s death, with no claimant remaining. Her rule was brief and violent. In 40, she had Teamfeill Baeldful murdered at Themoor. That same year, the Trostead Compact declared war on the Charter over a long-standing grievance. The Compact defeated the Charter at Gaehollow in 40, killing 126, and took the settlement. The Charter was finished, landless, in 40. Peace was made between the powers in 41 after the collapse of the Charter.

### The Trostead Compact, 42–48

The Trostead Compact’s rule, grounded in primogeniture, collapsed under internal betrayal and external revolt. A conspiracy by Laelldou Nerum against Paernso Feand was uncovered in 42, leading to Nerum’s execution. The power’s standing had fallen to nothing, allowing Geirnpei Shafts to rise against it in 43. Paernso Feand survived an open challenge from Nond Mea in 44, but killed the challenger at Trostead later that year.

Feand’s rule ended in 46 when Peansith Rae had him murdered at Micrag. Besveir Maleal, the named heir, took the seat after Skind Pesvi’s claim was rejected. The Compact was divided, with Skind Pesvi carrying off three places to Greater Themoor. Haeldkeind Nen challenged Besveir Maleal in 47, winning the seat and killing the former ruler at Trostead.

In 48, Peansith Rae challenged Haeldkeind Nen for the Compact and won. Rae took the seat by coup, and Haeldkeind Nen was cast out. The period ended with Peansith Rae holding the seat, having seized power through force rather than succession.

## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Skind Pesvi over Greater Themoor, 46–51

Skind Pesvi took control of Greater Themoor in 46 when the Trostead Compact divided, carrying off three places as the new power. In 47, Tha Vu and Dasould Ream returned from exile to take service with the state. The political landscape shifted in 48 when Dasould Ream married Mound Sith, binding Greater Themoor to the Trostead Compact. Tha Vu, captain of Greater Themoor, died in Themoor in 49.

In 50, Haeldkeind Nen returned from exile to take service with Greater Themoor. That same year, Greater Themoor demanded tribute of the Trostead Compact and was refused. The refusal was followed by public slights against the Compact in 51. Skind Pesvi’s rule continued through these tensions until the period ended in 51, marked by the birth of Feikein Geham to Skind Pesvi at Themoor.

### The rule of Peansith Rae over the Trostead Compact, 48–51

Peansith Rae seized the seat of the Trostead Compact in 48 through a coup against Haeldkeind Nen, who was cast out. The Compact had spent 229 silver buying goodwill earlier that year, but the effort was not believed. Rae’s accession was immediate, following his successful challenge to Nen.

In 49, Saerngein Wae was raised to steward of the Compact. By 50, Wae had won Voundfou Dealdwould away from the ruler. That same year, Greater Themoor demanded tribute from the Compact and was refused. A child, Rend Greall, was born to Vaswei Grand at Trostead.

The period ended in 51 when Greater Themoor publicly slighted the Trostead Compact. The Compact’s standing remained intact despite the refusal of tribute and the subsequent slight. Rae held the seat throughout this time.
