The period from 46 to 50 saw Greater Themoor consolidate its position through the absorption of territory and the reintegration of exiled personnel. At the start of the era, the Trostead Compact was divided, allowing Skind Pesvi to carry off three places as Greater Themoor. This expansion established the power’s territorial base, which was further secured by the return of key figures from exile. Tha Vu and Dasould Ream both returned from exile in 47 to take service with Greater Themoor, strengthening its internal ranks.

The political landscape shifted in 48 when Dasould Ream married Mound Sith, binding Greater Themoor to the Trostead Compact. This alliance marked a significant diplomatic development, linking the two powers through kinship. The period concluded in 50 with Haeldkeind Nen returning from exile to take service with Greater Themoor, continuing the trend of reclaiming lost personnel. The power ended the period with its territorial gains intact and its alliances formalized, having absorbed the resources of the divided Trostead Compact.
