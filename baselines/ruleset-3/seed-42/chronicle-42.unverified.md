The League also lost Laehiford and Hadale to the Kebarrow Compact. Internal strife culminated in the casting out of Reweld Wul and the seizure of the seat by Math Ham. The League finished as a landless power, its followers scattered.

A plague at Griwick in 26 killed 187 people and initiated a sickness that lasted four years, claiming 552 lives in total and driving 520 from their homes. The loss of Meigate to the Sworn Men of Meigate in 26 weakened the Compact, but the marriage of Leimmil Theall to Weavel Fel in 28 bound the two powers together. Thold Valmaer ruled until his death in 28, after which Thurnean Kourn, Leimmil Theall, Turaer Danpa, Tharhith Beamma, Veillpea Dourn, and Sou Dra held the seat in succession.
