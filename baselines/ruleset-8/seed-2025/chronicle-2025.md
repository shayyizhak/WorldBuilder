### The the Waeslefell Compact Aggression: the Waeslefell Compact against the Baesveireach Compact, 26–30

In 26, the Waeslefell Compact declared war on the Baesveireach Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam, where 36 died. In 27, the Waeslefell Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Thea Seam, where 30 died, and took the site from the Baesveireach Compact.

In 30, the Waeslefell Compact and the Baesveireach Compact made peace after 4 years, following the collapse of the Baesveireach Compact.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### The Deafil League, 2–35

The Deafil League spent thirty-four years bound to the Baesveireach Compact through four marriages, yet ended its existence defeated by the Waeslefell Compact. At the start of the period, the League held Skomere and maintained a fragile alliance with Waeslefell, but its standing deteriorated rapidly. By year 24, Kremoor rose against it as its authority fell very low, and by year 28, Kremoor broke away entirely as Free Kremoor. The League’s final years were marked by internal purges and a single war that it lost, resulting in the loss of Skomere and the scattering of its followers.

Feasleith Husbill held the seat until his death in year 11, after which Steapall Faedild took power by setting aside the named heir Bage Busba. Faedild’s rule was challenged openly by Fur Roundpoull in year 23; Faedild lost the seat and was cast out. Fur Roundpoull then ruled until year 28, when Sealdvern Geitoun challenged him and took the seat by coup. Geitoun’s rule lasted only one year before he was cast out for an attempted murder on Ground Raeskur. Deththou Keis then took the seat by setting aside the named heir Touthseand Gourses, holding power until the League’s end.

The League sent eight raids against the Waeslefell Compact between years 8 and 31. Two raids in years 8 and 9 carried off plunder from Waeslefell and Weisfaewick respectively. One raid in year 10 got through to Fa Cut but took nothing. Five subsequent raids in years 17, 18, 19, 30, and 31 were beaten off. The League suffered four raids from the Waeslefell Compact in years 8, 9, 18, and 19, all of which carried off plunder from Kremoor and Skomere. In year 34, the Waeslefell Compact declared war on the League over a long-standing grievance, broke its alliance, and defeated the League at Skomere, killing 179 and taking the settlement. The League was finished in year 34, landless, with its last four followers scattering.

### The Waeslefell Compact, 22–41

The Waeslefell Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by a violent succession and a protracted war that ended in territorial gain but internal collapse. The period began with Vaell Foull holding the seat, a rule that ended when Ground Raeskur had him murdered at Skomere in 24. Ground Raeskur took the seat by right of birth and held it for nine years, during which the Compact engaged in a war with the Baesveireach Compact that lasted four years. The Compact lost one battle at Thea Seam in 26 but won three others, taking Thea Seam and Baesveireach from its enemy. Ground Raeskur’s rule ended in 32 when Fur Roundpoull challenged him openly and won the seat.

Fur Roundpoull ruled for one year before dying in Waeslefell in 33. Rontaen Sin took the seat by setting aside the claim of the named heir Rearn Thill, dividing the Compact and losing Baesveireach to Greater Baesveireach. Rontaen Sin was murdered by Nounmam Mith in 36. Deththou Keis took the seat by right of birth but was cast out in 37 when Nounmam Mith won a coup. Nounmam Mith held the seat until the end of the period. Throughout these years, the Compact sent two raids that carried off plunder and suffered nine raids, all of which were beaten off. It fought five battles, winning four and losing one.

The Compact’s standing deteriorated significantly. In 35, Thea Seam rose against it because its standing had fallen to nothing. Six years of hunger at Thea Seam killed 23 people and caused 60 to flee. The Compact declared war on the Deafil League in 34, defeating it and taking Skomere, which led to the Deafil League’s destruction. It also made peace with the Baesveireach Compact after that power collapsed. Nine people were courted away from its rulers. Four people were cast out, three were murdered from within, and one was declared outlaw. The period ended with Nounmam Mith still holding the seat, but the Compact had lost two places and seen its internal cohesion break down.

## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Til Saeld over Free Kremoor, 49–51

Til Saeld assumed the seat of Free Kremoor in 49, following the casting out of Nounmam Mith. Mith’s attempt on the life of Woum Teldlei had failed and been traced back to him, leading to his expulsion. With no claimant remaining, Saeld was raised from Kremoor to take the seat.

The period ended in 51 when Free Kremoor’s raid on Fa Cut was beaten off.

### The rule of Haeth Tha over the Waeslefell Compact, 51–51

In year 51, Ronith Wasnur, the ruler of the Waeslefell Compact, died in Waeslefell. Haeth Tha contested the claim of Thraebaern Ramdem, the named heir, to the seat of the Waeslefell Compact. Haeth Tha took the seat, setting aside the claim of Thraebaern Ramdem.

Thraebaern Ramdem was cast out of the Waeslefell Compact. In the same year, a raid by Free Kremoor on Fa Cut was beaten off.
