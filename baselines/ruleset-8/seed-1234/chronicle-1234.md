# A history of the Rendfeall Vale

Seed 1234, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The Long Quarrel over Pei Delve: the Trostead Compact against House Buldbei, 9–11

The Trostead Compact declared war on House Buldbei in year 9, seeking control of Pei Delve. House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Compact at that site in the same year, with 23 dead. The Compact suffered another defeat at Pei Delve in year 10, resulting in 18 dead.

The conflict lasted two years. In year 11, the Trostead Compact and House Buldbei made peace, both sides exhausted.

### The War of the Galweall League's Grudge: the Galweall League against the Trostead Compact, 19–21

The Galweall League declared war on the Trostead Compact in 19, citing a long-standing grievance. The Trostead Compact defeated the Galweall League at Geirnpei Shafts in 19, where 19 people died.

In 20, Lald Neirntean had Kesfound Thrael murdered at Themoor. Later that year, the Galweall League and its allies defeated the Trostead Compact at Geirnpei Shafts, where 16 people died. The Galweall League took Geirnpei Shafts from the Trostead Compact.

The Galweall League and the Trostead Compact made peace in 21 after 2 years, both sides exhausted.

### House Buldbei, 3–22

The period from 3 to 22 was defined by internal instability and a persistent, though ultimately unsuccessful, conflict with the Trostead Compact. House Buldbei began with Skengaer Tharsoull holding the seat, but his rule ended quickly when he was killed by Trurnur Draeltheas in year 7. The seat passed through four rulers in twenty years, with an average tenure of 3.8 years. The house faced four years of hunger at Pei Delve, which killed 41 people and drove 60 from their homes. Despite these internal crises, House Buldbei won both battles fought against the Trostead Compact at Pei Delve in years 9 and 10.

Violence and betrayal marked the succession of power. Trurnur Draeltheas took the seat by coup after killing Skengaer Tharsoull, but was himself murdered by Lall Nealdgum in year 9. Kesfound Thrael then took the seat by the strongest claim, only to be cast out by Lall Nealdgum in year 12. Two rulers were cast out and two were killed during this period. Threth Kruth, who had previously held the seat, was declared outlaw after his conspiracy against Kesfound Thrael was uncovered in year 10. The standing of the house fell to nothing in year 9, prompting Pei Delve to rise against it.

The conflict with the Trostead Compact began in year 9 when the Compact declared war on House Buldbei for Pei Delve. The Compact broke its alliance with House Buldbei that same year. House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Compact at Pei Delve in year 9, killing 23, and again in year 10, killing 18. Peace was made after two years of war. House Buldbei sent four raids during this period: one against Trostead in year 4 was beaten off; raids on Pellweagate in year 10 and Geirnpei Shafts in year 11 got through but took nothing; and a raid on Trostead in year 20 carried off 32 grain and killed 26. Lall Nealdgum held the seat at the end of the period.

### The Rising of Pellweagate, 16–37

The Rising of Pellweagate spent its twenty-two years in a state of persistent failure, defined by external rejection and internal collapse. It began in year 16 when Veith Hei took the seat after Pellweagate broke from the Trostead Compact. The period was marked by a sequence of six raids, all of which were beaten off, and four years of hunger that killed 173 people. The power ended in year 36 when the Galweall League took Pellweagate and the Rising was finished, its last four followers scattering.

Veith Hei held the seat until year 32, a rule punctuated by famine and failed aggression. Hunger struck in years 19, 23, 27, and 30, killing 27, 55, 53, and 38 people respectively. During this time, the Rising sent out raids on Trostead in 19, Gaehollow in 31, Geirnpei Shafts in 32, Themoor in 33, Pei Delve in 34, and Trostead again in 35; every single one was beaten off. The internal stability of the rule eroded as exiles returned to service, including Beasre Feanwoul and Find Bound in year 26, and Besveir Maleal in year 33. Despite Find Bound marrying Draemo Grirn in year 27 to bind the Rising to the Trostead Compact, he challenged Veith Hei openly in year 32. Find Bound won, took the seat, and Veith Hei was cast out.

Find Bound’s rule lasted only four years before the Rising faced its end. In year 34, the Rising swore an alliance with House Buldbei, but this did not prevent its destruction. In year 36, the Galweall League declared war over a long-standing grievance, broke its alliance with the Rising, and defeated it at Pellweagate, where 136 people died. The Galweall League took Pellweagate from the Rising, and the Rising of Pellweagate was finished, landless, with its last four followers scattering. Peace was made between the Galweall League and the Rising in year 37, after the collapse of the Rising.

### The Galweall League, 42–51

The Galweall League’s decade was defined by internal collapse and violent succession. Its standing fell to nothing in 42, triggering a rebellion at Pei Delve, and it ended in 51 with Greimfourn Goul holding the seat after killing Mound Sith. The power began the period at war with House Buldbei, a conflict that lasted one year and ended with Buldbei’s destruction and the League taking Micrag. After that war, the League faced no external battles, but its internal stability vanished. Eight people held the seat in ten years, with the position passing seven times. Five rulers were killed, two were cast out, and one remained in power at the end.

Keandtei Taegro seized the seat in 42 through a coup against Lan Thraestun, who was cast out. Taegro’s rule lasted only a year before he was murdered by Del Monbirn at Trostead in 43. Veith Hei took the seat that same year by election, setting aside the claim of Haeldkeind Nen, who was cast out. Veith Hei’s tenure ended in 45 when Nond Mea challenged him openly and won. Nond Mea ruled for two years, surviving conspiracies by Del Monbirn and Vaswei Grand, both of whom were cast out. In 47, Rusund Gilteil challenged Nond Mea, won, and took the seat by coup, killing Nond Mea at Themoor.

Rusund Gilteil was murdered by Was Leithwon at Themoor in 48. Was Leithwon then took the seat by election, setting aside Peansith Rae, who was cast out. Was Leithwon’s rule was short; he was cast out in 49 after a conspiracy was uncovered. Varnnael Wansen took the seat by election in 48, but was murdered by Rend Greall at Themoor in 50. Mound Sith took the seat by election in 50. Greimfourn Goul challenged Mound Sith openly in 51, won, and killed him at Themoor, ending the period as the ruler. Throughout the decade, fifteen people were courted away from the ruler, reflecting a persistent pattern of defection.

### House Buldbei, 43–43

The period of 43 marked the final collapse of House Buldbei, ending its rule through the mechanism of strongest succession. The house’s standing ended abruptly as it was defeated by the Galweall League, a power that operated by election.

In 43, the Galweall League and House Buldbei made peace, a diplomatic conclusion that followed the collapse of House Buldbei after one year of conflict. This peace settlement formalized the end of the house’s power.
