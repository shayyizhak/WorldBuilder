# A history of the Naesdeind Vale

Seed 1, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Wougate Crown's Grudge: the Wougate Crown against the Reswoumark Crown, 5–7

The Wougate Crown declared war on the Reswoumark Crown in year 5 over Wei Seam. In that same year, the Wougate Crown and its allies defeated the Reswoumark Crown at Wei Seam, with 29 dead. The Wougate Crown took Wei Seam from the Reswoumark Crown as a result of the defeat.

In year 6, the Wougate Crown and its allies defeated the Reswoumark Crown at Reswoumark, with 198 dead. The Wougate Crown took Reswoumark from the Reswoumark Crown following this victory.

In year 7, the Wougate Crown and the Reswoumark Crown made peace after 2 years of conflict, following the collapse of the Reswoumark Crown.

### The House Wem Aggression: House Wem against the Wougate Crown, 22–26

House Wem declared war on the Wougate Crown in 22, citing a long-standing grievance. The Wougate Crown defeated House Wem at Thu Pits in 22, with 24 dead. The Wougate Crown defeated House Wem at Thu Pits again in 23, with 20 dead.

In 24, House Wem and its allies defeated the Wougate Crown at Thu Pits, with 16 dead. House Wem took Thu Pits from the Wougate Crown in 24. In 25, House Wem and its allies defeated the Wougate Crown at Wei Seam, with 15 dead. House Wem took Wei Seam from the Wougate Crown in 25.

House Wem and the Wougate Crown made peace in 26 after 4 years, both sides exhausted.

### The Reswoumark Crown, 4–7

The period from year 4 to 7 saw the Reswoumark Crown collapse under the weight of famine and war. It began as a power holding territory at Wei Seam and Reswoumark, but ended as a landless entity with its followers scattered. The decline was driven by a famine at Wei Seam that killed six people and shortened grain supplies, followed by a second wave of famine that killed thirteen more and forced thirty-one to abandon the place. These hardships preceded a declaration of war by the Wougate Crown, which sought control of Wei Seam.

In year 5, the Wougate Crown and its allies defeated the Reswoumark Crown at Wei Seam, killing twenty-nine and taking the site. The conflict continued into year 6, when the Wougate Crown and its allies defeated the Reswoumark Crown again at Reswoumark, killing 198 and taking the settlement. During this same year, internal violence marked the end of the ruler Diska Louth, who was killed by Fornhir Threi at Reswoumark. The Reswoumark Crown was finished, becoming landless as its last four followers scattered.

Peace was made in year 7 between the Wougate Crown and the Reswoumark Crown after two years of war, following the collapse of the Reswoumark Crown. The power that had held Wei Seam and Reswoumark was destroyed, its rule ending with the scattering of its remaining supporters.

### The Second Crown of Reswoumark, 21–51

The period from 21 to 51 saw the Second Crown of Reswoumark begin as a breakaway from the Wougate Crown and end in military defeat. Skobas Frul established the new power in 21 by taking two places from the Wougate Crown. The new state survived its early years through an alliance with its former parent in 29, but internal stability was fragile. Skobas Frul died in 30, and Mith Thres took the seat. Mith Thres’s rule was short and violent; he attempted to kill Lea Dae in 40 and was cast out for that act.

Difearn Kaerheald, the named heir, took the seat after Kurnsa Bealldo’s claim was rejected in 40. Difearn Kaerheald held the seat for two years before being killed by Kurnsa Bealldo in 42. The standing of the Second Crown of Reswoumark fell to nothing after this killing, and Haelabarrow rose against it in the same year. Thraellgul Thalgin took the seat by right of birth in 42. His rule ended when he attempted to kill Kurnsa Bealldo in 47 and was cast out.

Voun Woldlom took the seat by right of birth in 47. He held the seat until 51, when he also attempted to kill Kurnsa Bealldo and was cast out. Greandoul Krean took the seat by right of birth in 51. The period ended with the Second Crown of Reswoumark defeated by House Wem at Haelabarrow in 51, with 219 dead. The power had suffered four raids beaten off and three raids suffered, and endured two years of sickness that killed 400 people.

### The Sworn Men of Rucrag, 22–50

The Sworn Men of Rucrag spent twenty-nine years defined by a cycle of internal instability and external conflict with House Wem. The period began with the break from House Wem in 22, when Difearn Kaerheald took the seat. The power was shaped by the return of eight exiles who filled its ranks, but also by a succession of short, violent rules. Difearn Kaerheald held the seat until 30, when Tommarn Ri won an open challenge. Lea Dae then murdered Tommarn Ri in 35, but Naenddaem Houllteind retained the seat through election. Naenddaem was cast out in 36 after an attempt on Koldfaem Trir failed, and Greandoul Krean took the seat. Greandoul’s rule ended in 48 after an attempt on Kurnsa Bealldo failed, leading to his exile. Threihoull Thild took the seat, was killed by Kurnsa Bealldo in 49, and Skove Pull succeeded him. Skove Pull was also killed by Kurnsa Bealldo in 50, and Bi Binseall took the seat at the end of the period.

The Sworn Men of Rucrag engaged in five raids, four of which were beaten off. A raid on Thu Pits in 33 was beaten off. Raids on Wei Seam and Haelabarrow in 38 were beaten off. A raid on Broldtemoor in 44 was beaten off. Only the raid on Wougate in 42 carried off 53 grain and killed 27. The power suffered one raid on Rucrag in 47 by House Wem, which took nothing. A plague at Rucrag in 37 killed 121 people. Marriages tied the power to House Wem in 26 and the Wougate Crown in 27. Lea Dae courted Bi Binseall away in 34.

The quarrel with House Wem dominated the end of the period. House Wem declared war in 48 and broke its alliance. House Wem defeated the Sworn Men of Rucrag at Rucrag in 48, killing 178. House Wem defeated them again at Rucrag in 49, killing 148. Peace was made in 50 after two years of war. The Sworn Men of Rucrag finished the period with Bi Binseall holding the seat, having lost two battles and endured a plague.

### The Wougate Crown, 22–41

The period from 22 to 41 saw the Wougate Crown endure a war with House Wem, survive a plague, and lose two places. Seven people held the seat, four cast out, two killed, one still holding at the end. The seat passed six times. The Wougate Crown fought four battles against House Wem, winning two and losing two. It sent three raids, all beaten off. It suffered one raid. It ordered three killings. It lost two places. It endured two years of sickness killing 290. It made three marriages.

In 22, House Wem broke its alliance and declared war. Thrir Pondsurn challenged Lea Dae for the Wougate Crown and won. Thrir Pondsurn took the seat by coup. Lea Dae was cast out. The Wougate Crown defeated House Wem at Thu Pits, 24 dead. In 23, Thrir Pondsurn’s attempt on Tin Kaes failed. Thrir Pondsurn was cast out. The Wougate Crown defeated House Wem at Thu Pits, 20 dead. Tommarn Ri contested the claim of Haepin Maerba. Tommarn Ri took the seat, setting aside Haepin Maerba. Haepin Maerba was cast out. In 24, Tommarn Ri’s attempt on Tin Kaes failed. Tommarn Ri was cast out. House Wem and its allies defeated the Wougate Crown at Thu Pits, 16 dead. House Wem took Thu Pits. Sillmaem Freith took the seat by right of birth.

Sillmaem Freith had Tin Kaes murdered at Broldtemoor in 25. House Wem and its allies defeated the Wougate Crown at Wei Seam, 15 dead. House Wem took Wei Seam. In 26, Sillmaem Freith had Goul Geall murdered at Broldtemoor. House Wem and the Wougate Crown made peace. Kros Ranu’s conspiracy against Sillmaem Freith was uncovered. Kros Ranu was put to death. In 27, Sillmaem Freith had Rein Ga murdered at Broldtemoor. In 28, Pondfern Lull married Koldfaem Trir, binding the Wougate Crown to House Wem. Plague broke out at Wougate, taking 95. In 29, the plague killed 195 and 187 fled. The Wougate Crown’s raid on Thu Pits was beaten off. The Second Crown of Reswoumark and the Wougate Crown swore an alliance.

In 31, Kurnsa Bealldo had Sillmaem Freith murdered at Wougate. Sillmaem Freith was killed. Neildpoun Porn took the seat by right of birth. In 32, Voun Woldlom married Mith Thres, binding the Wougate Crown to the Second Crown of Reswoumark. The Wougate Crown’s raid on Wei Seam was beaten off. In 33, the Wougate Crown’s raid on Broldtemoor was beaten off. In 34, Kurnsa Bealldo’s conspiracy against Neildpoun Porn was uncovered. Kurnsa Bealldo was cast out. In 35, Thraellgul Thalgin challenged Neildpoun Porn openly, won, and took the seat. Neildpoun Porn was killed. In 36, Thraellgul Thalgin married Naerream Thrurei, binding the Wougate Crown to House Wem.

In 38, Thraellgul Thalgin’s attempt on Koldfaem Trir failed. Thraellgul Thalgin was cast out. House Wem raided Wougate, carrying off 33 grain and killing 28. Voun Woldlom took the seat by right of birth. The period ended in 41.

### The rule of Greandoul Krean over the Second Crown of Reswoumark, 51–51

In year 51, House Wem declared war on the Second Crown of Reswoumark over a long-standing grievance, causing trade between the two powers to break down. Within the Crown, Voun Woldlom’s attempt on the life of Kurnsa Bealldo failed and was traced back to him. As a result of this attempted murder, Voun Woldlom was cast out of the Second Crown of Reswoumark.

The war continued with House Wem defeating the Second Crown of Reswoumark at Haelabarrow, where 219 people died. Following the vacancy created by the casting out of Voun Woldlom, Greandoul Krean took the seat of the Second Crown of Reswoumark by right of birth.
