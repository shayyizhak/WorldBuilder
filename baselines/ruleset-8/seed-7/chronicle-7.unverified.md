## The Goummeidale Compact, 42–50

- `relative-time` — "…ed after three years. Faeth was put to death for the crime. The following year, Gu Veam attempted to kill Ma…" — replace "the following year" with the absolute year from the record, or drop the time reference
- `ambiguous-short-name` — two powers here are called "compact"; the short form says neither

The Goummeidale Compact’s rule in these years was defined by Maethhol Gondrur’s survival against a series of internal conspiracies that eroded the power’s standing until its collapse. At the start, in 42, the Compact faced a plague at Seithfaehollow that killed 103 people and a conspiracy by Seill Kreannae against Maethhol Gondrur. Kreannae’s plot was uncovered after four years, and he was cast out of the Compact. The plague continued into 43, killing 150 more and forcing 171 to flee Seithfaehollow.

In 46, another conspiracy by Thaeld Faeth against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered after three years. Faeth was put to death for the crime. The following year, Gu Veam attempted to kill Maethhol Gondrur; the attempt failed and was traced back, leading to Veam’s expulsion from the Compact. By 50, the Compact’s authority had weakened significantly. Gu Veam, who had already left, was declared an outlaw after a new conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered.

That same year, Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered after three years, and Teththeald was cast out of the Compact. The loss of internal cohesion allowed Sti Seam to rise against the Goummeidale Compact. The Compact’s standing had fallen to nothing, and it was destroyed by this uprising. Maethhol Gondrur remained the holder of the seat, but the power itself was finished.


## The rule of Maethhol Gondrur over the Goummeidale Compact, 40–51

- `out-of-order` — the killing of Brethneld Draer in 40 is what opened the seat, and is told after Maethhol Gondrur takes it

Maethhol Gondrur took the seat of the Goummeidale Compact in 40 after Seill Kreannae killed Brethneld Draer. The new ruler’s tenure began with a plague at Seithfaehollow that took 106 lives. In 42, the pestilence burned out after one year, only to break out again and take 103 more. Seill Kreannae’s conspiracy against Maethhol Gondrur was uncovered after four years, and Seill was cast out of the Goummeidale Compact.

The plague at Seithfaehollow continued into 43, killing 150 and forcing 171 to flee before burning out after two years. Thaeld Faeth won Fol Skeambol away from the ruler, prompting the Goummeidale Compact to spend 185 silver to buy back goodwill in 46. Thaeld’s own conspiracy was uncovered after three years, and he was put to death. Gu Veam’s attempt on Maethhol Gondrur failed and was traced back to Fol Skeambol; Gu was cast out of the Goummeidale Compact for attempted murder.

In 50, Gu Veam’s conspiracy was uncovered, and he was declared outlaw. Skein Teththeald’s conspiracy was also uncovered after three years, and he was cast out of the Goummeidale Compact. Sti Seam rose against the Goummeidale Compact, whose standing had fallen to nothing. The period ended in 51 with Maethhol Gondrur still holding the seat.
