# A history of the Rulllound Vale

Seed 7, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Goummeidale Compact's Grudge: the Goummeidale Compact against House Thream, 5–7

In year 5, the Goummeidale Compact declared war on House Thream over control of Sti Seam. The conflict began with a decisive engagement at that site, where the Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream, leaving 34 dead. The fighting continued at the same location in year 6, resulting in another defeat for House Thream and 28 more deaths. Following this second loss, the Goummeidale Compact took Sti Seam from House Thream.

The war lasted for two years. In year 7, the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream made peace, both sides exhausted by the conflict.

### The Long Quarrel over Kraeford: the Goummeidale Compact against the Kraeford Compact, 21–23

The Goummeidale Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact in 21, citing a long-standing grievance. In that same year, Goummeidale defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, where 110 people died, and took the settlement from them. In 22, Goummeidale defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, where 181 people died, and took Kraeford from them.

The conflict ended in 23 when the two powers made peace after two years, following the collapse of the Kraeford Compact.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### House Thream, 2–29

House Thream’s twenty-eight years ended in total destruction, a trajectory defined by the collapse of its alliances and the relentless pressure of the Goummeidale Compact. The period began with an alliance between House Thream and the Kraeford Compact, but this foundation eroded quickly. In year 3, the death of Freamteas Kraeth triggered a succession crisis resolved by Bran Nas, who took the seat by the strongest claim. Bran Nas’s rule was brief; he died in year 6, allowing Kokaes Threll to seize the seat by setting aside the named heir Ma Graltaeld. Kokaes Threll’s tenure was equally unstable, ending in his murder by Fous Bo in year 8. Vinbeil Real then took the seat, ruling until his death in year 17. Sen Tom succeeded him, only to be challenged and killed by Maethhol Gondrur in year 26. Maethhol Gondrur held the seat until House Thream was finished in year 28. Six people held the seat in these years, with an average tenure of 4.3 years.

The power’s decline was accelerated by its failure to maintain external bonds and its inability to withstand the Goummeidale Compact. A marriage in year 3 had bound House Thream to the Goummeidale Compact, but the Compact declared war on House Thream in year 5 over a grievance concerning Sti Seam. The Compact broke its alliance with House Thream that same year. House Thream suffered four battles against the Goummeidale Compact, all lost: at Sti Seam in year 5 with 34 dead, at Sti Seam in year 6 with 28 dead, at Loufell in year 27 with 123 dead, and at Loufell in year 28 with 103 dead. Consequently, House Thream lost two places: Sti Seam in year 6 and Loufell in year 28. The Compact raided Loufell in year 24, killing 18 but taking nothing. House Thream’s own raids were largely ineffective; of seven raids sent out, six were beaten off, and only one, against Seithfaehollow in year 5, carried off plunder.

Internal instability compounded these external defeats. House Thream experienced two years of hunger, killing 37 people in total: six at Sti Seam in year 4 and thirty-one at Loufell in year 23. Two people were cast out: Ma Graltaeld for the losing claim in year 6, and Fous Bo for conspiracy in year 10. Two exiles returned to service: Vinbeil Real in year 7 and Kraedren Waeld in year 22. Three people were courted away from the ruler. The period ended in year 29 with the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream making peace, but House Thream was already finished, its landless followers scattered.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 2–21

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 2 to 21 was defined by a cycle of internal purges and external wars that eroded its authority while it expanded its territory. The period began with the Compact holding Ra Cut, a site it had occupied in year 2. It ended with Freindmald Beindlea still holding the seat, having succeeded Kaell Frouspim after his death in year 21. Throughout these years, the Compact fought three battles, winning all of them, and launched two raids that carried off plunder. However, its internal stability was fragile; four people held the seat, with three killed and one remaining. The Compact suffered eight raids, most of which were beaten off, and faced two uprisings from places whose standing had fallen very low.

Sealoll Lael held the seat at the start, ruling by primogeniture. His rule was marked by a conspiracy uncovered in year 5, which led to the casting out of Vinbeil Real. In year 12, Sealoll Lael was murdered at Goummeidale by Thral Rei, who was subsequently cast out for his own conspiracy in year 13. Fous Bo took the seat by right of birth in year 12. His rule saw the Compact raid Kraeford in year 13, taking 40 grain and killing 28. Fous Bo was murdered at Goummeidale in year 19 by Kraedren Waeld, who was then cast out for conspiracy in year 20. Kaell Frouspim took the seat by right of birth in year 19. He was killed by Frouldtoun Dror in year 21, after which Freindmald Beindlea took the seat by right of birth. Three people were cast out for conspiracy against the seat, and three exiles returned to service.

The Compact’s external relations shifted from alliance to war. In year 3, Kaell Frouspim married Frouldtoun Dror, binding the Compact to the Kraeford Compact. In year 5, Freindmald Beindlea married Fous Bo, binding the Compact to House Thream. The Compact declared war on House Thream in year 5 over Sti Seam, breaking the alliance with Thream. It defeated House Thream at Sti Seam in year 5 and year 6, taking the site. Peace was made in year 7. In year 21, the Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact over a long-standing grievance, breaking the alliance. It defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow in year 21, taking the site. The Compact raided Seithfaehollow in year 16, carrying off 32 grain and killing 36. Ra Cut and Sti Seam rose against the Compact in years 13 and 18, respectively, due to its fallen standing. Hunger and sickness killed 364 people and caused 502 to flee their homes across the period.

### The Kraeford Compact, 2–23

The Kraeford Compact’s twenty-two years ended in total destruction, a trajectory defined by internal instability and a fatal war with the Goummeidale Compact. The period began with the Compact allied to House Thream, but its leadership was fragile. Skur Stas held the seat until his murder by Kraedren Waeld in year 5. Waeld’s subsequent challenge to the named heir, Stearnke Gullleith, was rejected by election, and Waeld was cast out. Gullleith then ruled until his death in year 9, after which Frouldtoun Dror took the seat by election. The Compact’s authority was further undermined when Thral Rei, an exile who had returned to service, conspired against Dror and was executed in year 18.

Military engagements were uniformly disastrous. The Compact sent five raids, four of which were beaten off and one that carried off plunder. It suffered three raids, all of which carried off plunder. The turning point came in year 21 when the Goummeidale Compact declared war over a long-standing grievance, breaking its alliance with the Compact. The Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, taking the settlement, and again at Kraeford, taking that city. The Compact was finished in year 22, its land lost and its followers scattered.

The period saw two years of hunger killing 74 people, and one marriage binding the Compact to House Thream. The war with the Goummeidale Compact lasted two years and resulted in two battles, both lost. The Compact’s rule passed twice, from Skur Stas to Stearnke Gullleith and then to Frouldtoun Dror. The power was destroyed by the Goummeidale Compact, which made peace in year 23 after the Compact’s collapse.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 22–41

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by violent succession and the elimination of external rivals. The period began with the murder of Freindmald Beindlea by Waeld Dould at Goummeidale, an act that triggered a cascade of internal purges and external conquests. The Compact emerged from the destruction of the Kraeford Compact and House Thream, securing the settlements of Kraeford and Loufell. It ended with Maethhol Gondrur holding the seat, having inherited it after the murder of Brethneld Draer, while the power itself remained intact despite a plague that killed 106 people at Seithfaehollow.

Internal stability was nonexistent. Ten people held the seat, passing it nine times through murder, coup, and open challenge. Waeld Dould killed Freindmald Beindlea in 22, allowing Frouldtoun Dror to take the seat by right of birth. Dror’s rule ended when he was cast out in 28 for an attempted murder on Maethhol Gondrur. Voumfaern Ki succeeded him but was cast out in 29 after losing an open challenge to Skullhein Threill. Threill was murdered in 30 by Vasgou Raend, who then took the seat by coup. Vasgou died in 34, and Wold Risla took the seat by birth. Risla was cast out in 35 after losing an open challenge to Brethneld Draer. Draer was murdered in 40 by Seill Kreannae, allowing Maethhol Gondrur to take the seat by birth. Six of the rulers were killed or cast out by their own people, and six people were courted away from the ruler.

Externally, the Compact expanded by destroying its neighbors. In 22, it defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, killing 181 and taking the settlement, which led to the Kraeford Compact’s end. In 27, it declared war on House Thream over a long-standing grievance. It defeated House Thream at Loufell in 27, killing 123, and again in 28, killing 103, taking Loufell and ending House Thream. The Compact sent one raid to Loufell in 24, which got through but took nothing. It suffered three raids, all beaten off. The quarrel with House Thream resulted in two battles, both won by the Compact.
