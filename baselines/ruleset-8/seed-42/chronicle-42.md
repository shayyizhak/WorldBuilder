### The Griwick Compact, 4–23

The Griwick Compact’s twenty-year span from 4 to 23 was defined by a war with the Wurn League that lasted five years and ended in exhaustion, a succession crisis that shattered its internal cohesion, and a steady erosion of its authority that allowed places to rise against it. The period began with the Compact raiding Kebarrow, a move driven by famine at Vea Lode, and ended with Thold Valmaer holding the seat after a murder, having taken power by the strongest claim. Four people held the seat during this time, with an average tenure of 4.7 years and a median of 5.5. The seat passed three times: Pouldrir Ho was cast out, Kei Leassel died, Witrou Breim was killed, and Thold Valmaer remained in power at the end.

The conflict with the Wurn League began in 5 when the Compact declared war for Threi Cut and broke its alliance with the League. The League defeated the Compact at Threi Cut in 5, killing 45, but the Compact reversed this in 6 by defeating the League at the same site, killing 35. It won again at Threi Cut in 7, killing 29, and took Threi Cut from the League. In 8, the Compact took Laehiford from the League after defeating them there, killing 107, and defeated them again at Hadale in 9, killing 101. The war ended in 11 after five years, with both sides exhausted. Throughout the period, the Compact fought five battles against the League, winning four and losing one.

Internal stability collapsed in 9 when Pouldrir Ho was cast out for attempted murder. Kei Leassel took the seat by defeating the named heir Turaer Danpa, who was also cast out. The Compact’s standing fell very low, and Vea Lode rose against it in 9. By 12, its standing had fallen to nothing, and Threi Cut rose against it. Kei Leassel ruled until his death in 17. Witrou Breim took the seat by rejecting Beirn Naem’s claim, and Beirn was cast out. Witrou Breim was murdered by Bu Rumpirn in 23, and Thold Valmaer took the seat by the strongest claim. The Compact sent six raids, all carrying off plunder, and suffered ten raids, six of which were beaten off. Hunger killed 43 people across twelve years of famine.

### The Kebarrow Compact, 22–40

The Kebarrow Compact’s nineteen-year tenure from 22 to 40 was defined by a relentless decline from a position of electoral authority to total dissolution. The period began with Sou Dra holding the seat, a ruler whose authority was immediately undermined by internal conspiracy and external aggression. The Compact’s standing eroded through a series of military defeats against the Griwick Compact, the loss of key territories, and the violent turnover of its leadership. By the end of the period, the power was finished, its land seized and its followers scattered.

Sou Dra’s rule was marked by instability and failed expansion. In 25, a raid on Griwick was beaten off, and a conspiracy by Turaer Danpa was uncovered, leading to Danpa’s execution. The Compact’s aggression continued in 26 with a successful raid on Laehiford, which brought 44 grain and killed 25, but this was met in kind when the Griwick Compact raided Kebarrow, taking 47 grain and killing 23. In 27, the Compact declared war on the Griwick Compact over a long-standing grievance, breaking a prior alliance. The war proved disastrous; the Compact was defeated at Vea Lode in 27 with 57 dead and again at Vea Lode in 28 with 48 dead. A raid on Threi Cut that year also failed to take anything. Sou Dra’s internal control slipped further as he ordered the murder of Thold Valmaer at Griwick in 28 and survived another conspiracy by Theald Va, who was executed.

The leadership of the Compact changed hands four times, each transition violent or forced. In 29, Tor Nathgoull challenged Sou Dra openly, won, and took the seat, casting Sou Dra out. Tor Nathgoull’s rule was short-lived; in 32, Throll Kell challenged him, won, and took the seat by coup, casting Tor Nathgoull out. Throll Kell held the seat until 35, when Beas Krouthea challenged him openly, won, and took the seat, casting Throll Kell out. Beas Krouthea’s rule ended in blood; in 38, Kou Peis murdered him at Kebarrow. During these years, the Compact suffered further losses. Meigate broke away in 29 to form the Meigate Covenant under Raes Go. A famine at Meigate in 26 had already killed 34 people. The Compact’s military fortunes remained poor, with raids on Hadale in 35 beaten off.

The final phase of the Compact saw it destroyed by the Griwick Compact. In 38, the Griwick Compact declared war on the Kebarrow Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Griwick Compact and its allies defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Kebarrow in 38 with 142 dead. Diweith Mound took the seat by election in 38 following Beas Krouthea’s death, but his rule was brief and treacherous; he attempted to murder Durnrin Drar in 39 and was cast out for attempted murder. The Griwick Compact and its allies defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Kebarrow again in 39 with 118 dead, then took Kebarrow from the Compact. The Kebarrow Compact was finished, landless, with its last 9 followers scattering. The Griwick Compact and the Kebarrow Compact made peace in 40, after the collapse of the Kebarrow Compact.

### The Meigate Covenant, 29–51

The Meigate Covenant’s twenty-three years from 29 to 51 were defined by a shift from isolation to entanglement, and finally to exhaustion. It began by breaking from the Kebarrow Compact in 29, with Raes Go taking the seat. The period opened with a series of failed raids against the Griwick Compact and the Kebarrow Compact, all beaten off. A famine at Meigate in 38 killed 61 people and left grain short by 7. The Covenant’s standing fluctuated as it occupied Vea Lode in 41, only to lose it to a rising in 45 when its standing had fallen to nothing. Raes Go was cast out in 44 after losing an open challenge to Diweith Mound, who was himself killed in 46. Drarka Draernthun took the seat that year, setting aside the claim of the named heir Tor Nathgoull.

The Covenant’s external relations turned volatile. It declared war on the Griwick Compact in 47 over a long-standing grievance, breaking its alliance with them. The Covenant raided Griwick, carrying off 23 grain and killing 16, but was defeated at Threi Cut later that year with 12 dead. It was defeated again at Threi Cut in 48 with 10 dead. Peace was made in 49 after two years of war, leaving both sides exhausted. A marriage between Hehum Skul and Ta Poveil in 50 bound the Meigate Covenant to the Griwick Compact. Throughout the period, the seat passed from one person to another two times. Three people held the seat: Raes Go, Diweith Mound, and Drarka Draernthun. One was cast out, one was killed, and one was still holding.

Internal stability was undermined by repeated exiles and returns. Eleven exiles returned to take service with the Meigate Covenant during these years. Three people were courted away from its ruler: Heillvar Maer won Nourn Dem away in 41, Heillvar Maer won Drarka Draernthun away in 42, and Drarka Draernthun won Kaes Rou away in 46. Hunger afflicted Vea Lode multiple times, killing 68 people in all and causing 60 to flee their homes. The Covenant sent eight raids, four of which were beaten off and four of which carried off plunder. It suffered two raids from the Griwick Compact. The period ended with the Meigate Covenant still holding the seat, having fought two battles, both lost to the Griwick Compact.

### The Vea Lode Covenant, 29–39

The Vea Lode Covenant began in year 29 when Vea Lode broke from the Griwick Compact, with Mothgall Vafarn taking the seat. The new power faced immediate hardship, as a famine at Vea Lode in year 30 left grain short and 11 dead. The crisis deepened in subsequent years, with the famine persisting through year 33. In year 31, 13 more died and 32 abandoned the place. In year 32, 9 died and 22 left. In year 33, 7 died and 30 abandoned the place.

Internal strife followed the external pressure. In year 33, Sou Dra had Mothgall Vafarn murdered at Vea Lode. Sou Dra then took the seat of the Vea Lode Covenant by election. The Covenant turned outward, raiding Griwick in year 34, carrying off 36 grain and killing 20. In year 35, it raided Laehiford, carrying off 44 grain and killing 28.

Bu Rumpirn, who had returned from exile in year 30, acted against the ruler. In year 36, Bu Rumpirn had Sou Dra murdered at Vea Lode. Bu Rumpirn took the seat by election. He ruled until he died at 60 in Vea Lode in year 39. The Vea Lode Covenant came to an end in year 39; its 1 place fell to no one.
