# A history of the Rendfeall Vale

Seed 1234, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The Long Quarrel over Pei Delve: the Trostead Compact against House Buldbei, 9–11

The Trostead Compact declared war on House Buldbei in year 9, seeking control of Pei Delve. House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Compact at that site in the same year, with 23 dead. The Compact suffered another defeat at Pei Delve in year 10, with 18 dead.

The conflict lasted two years. In year 11, the Trostead Compact and House Buldbei made peace, both sides exhausted.

### The War of the Galweall League's Grudge: the Galweall League against the Trostead Compact, 19–21

In 19, the Galweall League declared war on the Trostead Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Trostead Compact defeated the Galweall League at Geirnpei Shafts in the same year, with 19 dead.

In 20, Lald Neirntean had Kesfound Thrael murdered at Themoor. Later that year, the Galweall League and its allies defeated the Trostead Compact at Geirnpei Shafts, with 16 dead. The Galweall League took Geirnpei Shafts from the Trostead Compact.

In 21, the Galweall League and the Trostead Compact made peace after 2 years, both sides exhausted.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### The Galweall League, 2–21

The Galweall League’s twenty-year span from 2 to 21 was defined by a cycle of violent retaliation against the Trostead Compact and internal purges that reshaped its leadership. The period opened with a marriage alliance to House Buldbei and ended with another, but the core dynamic was a war of attrition. The League suffered four raids and lost one battle, yet it also inflicted damage and ultimately reclaimed territory. The seat passed once, from Lald Neirntean, who was killed, to Threth Kruth, who held it at the end.

Lald Neirntean consolidated power through murder, ordering the killings of Faellwil Na in 4, Paepu Noth in 17, and Kesfound Thrael in 20. He returned five exiles to service: Gall Brolllea, Frernli Peimeath, Threth Kruth, Neallseis Staend, and Kesfound Thrael. The League launched five raids against the Trostead Compact. Three succeeded in taking plunder from Geirnpei Shafts in 6 and Pellweagate in 7 and 16. One raid on Trostead in 5 was beaten off, and one on Geirnpei Shafts in 15 got through but took nothing. The Compact retaliated with four raids, taking grain from Gaehollow in 3 and 16, and Themoor in 4 and 18.

In 19, the League declared war on the Compact. The Compact defeated the League at Geirnpei Shafts, killing 19. The League’s allies defeated the Compact at the same site in 20, killing 16, and the League took Geirnpei Shafts from the Compact. In 21, Paernso Feand had Lald Neirntean murdered at Themoor. The League and Compact made peace after two years of war. Threth Kruth took the seat by election. Two people were courted away from the ruler: Threth Kruth won Dasould Ream in 19 and Hea Brar in 20.

### House Buldbei, 3–22

The twenty-year period from 3 to 22 was defined by internal instability and a persistent, exhausting conflict with the Trostead Compact. House Buldbei, which succeeded by the strongest claim, saw five different rulers in succession, with the seat passing four times. The house began the period holding the seat but ended it with Lall Nealdgum still in power, having survived two coups and a war. The period opened with famine at Pei Delve and closed with a successful raid on Trostead, but the intervening years were marked by rapid turnover of leadership and a standing that fell to nothing before rising again.

The early years were consumed by famine at Pei Delve, which killed 41 people and drove 60 from their homes between years 3 and 8. This weakness allowed Trurnur Draeltheas to murder Skengaer Tharsoull, the ruler of House Buldbei, in year 7. Threth Kruth took the seat next by the strongest claim, but Trurnur Draeltheas challenged and won, taking the seat by coup in year 8 and casting out Threth Kruth. Lall Nealdgum then murdered Trurnur Draeltheas in year 9, and Kesfound Thrael took the seat by the strongest claim. In that same year, the Trostead Compact declared war on House Buldbei for Pei Delve and broke its alliance with them. Pei Delve rose against House Buldbei, whose standing had fallen to nothing, but House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Trostead Compact at Pei Delve, killing 23.

The conflict with the Trostead Compact continued into year 10, when House Buldbei and its allies defeated them again at Pei Delve, killing 18. During this time, Threth Kruth’s conspiracy against Kesfound Thrael was uncovered, and he was declared outlaw. House Buldbei sent out four raids: one against Trostead in year 4 was beaten off; raids on Pellweagate in year 10 and Geirnpei Shafts in year 11 got through but took nothing; and a raid on Trostead in year 20 carried off 32 grain and killed 26. Lall Nealdgum challenged Kesfound Thrael in year 12, won, and took the seat by coup, casting out Threth Kruth. The war with the Trostead Compact ended in peace in year 11 after two years of fighting. The period ended with Lall Nealdgum still holding the seat, having survived the internal purges and the external war.

### The Rising of Pellweagate, 16–37

The Rising of Pellweagate spent its twenty-two years in a state of persistent failure, defined by external rejection and internal collapse. It began in year 16 when Veith Hei took the seat after Pellweagate broke from the Trostead Compact. The period was marked by a sequence of six raids, all of which were beaten off, and four years of hunger that killed 173 people. The power ended in year 36 when the Galweall League took Pellweagate and the Rising was finished, its last four followers scattering.

Veith Hei held the seat until year 32, a rule punctuated by famine and failed aggression. Between years 19 and 30, four famines struck Pellweagate, killing 27, 55, 53, and 38 people respectively. During this time, the Rising sent out raids on Trostead, Gaehollow, Geirnpei Shafts, Themoor, Pei Delve, and Trostead again; every single one was beaten off. In year 27, Find Bound married Draemo Grirn, binding the Rising to the Trostead Compact, yet this alliance did not prevent further isolation. In year 30, Find Bound won Beasre Feanwoul away from Veith Hei, signaling the erosion of the ruler’s authority.

In year 32, Find Bound challenged Veith Hei openly for the seat, won, and cast Veith Hei out. Find Bound’s rule was brief and equally unsuccessful. In year 34, the Rising swore an alliance with House Buldbei, but this did not halt its decline. In year 36, the Galweall League declared war over a long-standing grievance, broke its alliance with the Rising, and defeated it at Pellweagate, where 136 people died. The Galweall League took Pellweagate from the Rising, and the Rising of Pellweagate was finished.

### The Galweall League, 22–41

The Galweall League spent twenty years defined by internal instability and external expansion. Its leadership changed hands eight times, with nine individuals holding the seat in total. The period began with Threth Kruth ruling, who died in 30, and ended with Lan Thraestun taking the seat in 41. The League fought four battles, winning all of them, and took three places: Trostead from the Trostead Compact in 29, Pei Delve from House Buldbei in 32, and Pellweagate from the Rising of Pellweagate in 36. It also destroyed two powers, the Trostead Compact and the Rising of Pellweagate, while enduring a long-running quarrel with House Buldbei that resulted in two battles.

Threth Kruth held the seat until his death in 30. During his rule, the League broke its alliance with the Trostead Compact and declared war in 29 over a long-standing grievance. The League defeated the Compact at Trostead, killing 167, and took the settlement. The Compact was finished, its last seven followers scattering. In 30, Del Monbirn had Tha Vu murdered at Trostead. Draemo Grirn took the seat from the named heir Hibrei Fou by election in 30, casting Hibrei out. Thimseild Soldrind challenged Draemo openly in 31, lost, and was cast out. Besveir Maleal challenged and defeated Draemo in 32, taking the seat and casting Draemo out.

Besveir’s rule was short. Thimseild challenged him in 32, won, and took the seat by coup, casting Besveir out. Dreamwein Vous challenged Thimseild in 33, won, and took the seat, casting Thimseild out. Dreamwein had Lall Nealdgum murdered at Micrag in 34. Bathwa Bea attempted to kill Dreamwein in 35, failed, and was cast out. Thaham Geth challenged Dreamwein in 36, won, and took the seat by coup, casting Dreamwein out. The League defeated the Rising of Pellweagate at Pellweagate, killing 136, and took the settlement. The Rising was finished, its last four followers scattering.

Thaham Geth ruled until 38. Laelldou Nerum challenged him in 38, won, and took the seat, killing Thaham at Themoor. Lurntu Skondbea challenged Laelldou in 39, won, and took the seat, killing Laelldou at Themoor. The League raided Micrag in 39, killing 29 but taking nothing. Find Bound challenged Lurntu in 40, lost, and was cast out. Beasre Feanwoul attempted to kill Lurntu in 41, failed, and was cast out. Lan Thraestun challenged Lurntu in 41, won, and took the seat, killing Lurntu at Themoor. Beasre was declared outlaw for conspiracy in 41. The period ended with Lan holding the seat.

### The Trostead Compact, 22–30

The Trostead Compact’s nine-year existence from 22 to 30 was defined by internal instability and external defeat. The power began with a failed raid on Geirnpei Shafts in 22 and ended with its destruction by the Galweall League in 29. Three people held the seat during this period, each ruling for a median of two years. The Compact’s rule was characterized by violent succession, a brief alliance that collapsed into war, and the loss of its capital.

Vun Doultheam held the seat from before 22 until his death in 25. His rule ended when Tha Vu had him murdered at Trostead. Besveir Maleal took the seat by right of birth in 25. His rule was short-lived; in 27, Del Monbirn challenged him openly, won, and took the seat. Besveir Maleal was cast out as the losing side of that challenge. Tha Vu, who had orchestrated the earlier murder, was also cast out in 27 after his conspiracy against Del Monbirn was uncovered.

Del Monbirn ruled from 27 until the Compact’s end. In 28, Keandtei Taegro married Thimseild Soldrind, binding the Compact to the Galweall League. This alliance did not prevent conflict. In 28, a Compact raid on Gaehollow was beaten off. In 29, the Galweall League declared war over a long-standing grievance and broke the alliance. The Galweall League and its allies defeated the Compact at Trostead, killing 167 people, and took the settlement. The Trostead Compact was finished, its last seven followers scattering. Peace was made in 30.

### House Buldbei, 23–42

House Buldbei’s tenure from 23 to 42 was defined by a rapid collapse from internal stability to total destruction, driven by a persistent war with the Galweall League and a series of fatal leadership failures. The period began with Lall Nealdgum holding the seat, a rule that ended only when he was killed by Dreamwein Vous in 34. The house’s standing had already deteriorated significantly, marked by Pei Delve rising against it in 28 when its standing fell to nothing. This loss of authority coincided with severe famine at Micrag in 26 and 27, which killed 264 people and caused 282 to flee.

The conflict with the Galweall League escalated after the League declared war in 32 over Pei Delve. House Buldbei fought three battles against the League, all of which were lost: at Pei Delve in 32 with 12 dead, at Micrag in 33 with 235 dead, and at Micrag in 42 with 191 dead. The League took Pei Delve in 32 and Micrag in 42. During this time, House Buldbei sent six raids, five of which were beaten off and one that carried off plunder from Themoor in 32. It suffered one raid on Micrag in 39, which got through but took nothing. The house’s internal cohesion fractured as Hibrei Fou took the seat in 34 by the strongest claim, only to be murdered by Thimseild Soldrind in 39.

Thimseild Soldrind took the seat in 39 after rejecting Bathwa Bea’s claim, but his rule was short-lived. He was cast out in 42 for attempted murder, ending the house’s line. The Galweall League defeated House Buldbei at Micrag in 42 and took the settlement, after which the house was finished, its last follower scattering. Throughout the period, three people held the seat, and three were cast out or killed. The quarrel with the Galweall League persisted through three battles, and the house lost two places, Pei Delve and Micrag, before its final dissolution.

### The Galweall League, 42–51

The Galweall League’s decade was defined by the rapid turnover of its leadership and the collapse of its external standing. At the start of 42, the League held the seat through Lan Thraestun, but his rule ended immediately when Keandtei Taegro challenged him and won. Taegro took the seat by coup, casting out Thraestun. This internal seizure coincided with a war against House Buldbei. The League defeated Buldbei at Micrag, killing 191, and took the settlement. House Buldbei was finished, leaving the League landless in its rival. However, the League’s own standing fell to nothing, allowing Pei Delve to rise against it.

The seat passed seven times in ten years, with five rulers killed and two cast out. Keandtei Taegro ruled for one year before being murdered by Del Monbirn at Trostead. Veith Hei took the seat by election, setting aside the named heir Haeldkeind Nen, who was cast out. Veith Hei was then cast out after losing an open challenge to Nond Mea. Nond Mea’s rule ended when Rusund Gilteil challenged him, won, and killed him at Themoor. Rusund Gilteil was murdered by Was Leithwon at Themoor. Varnnael Wansen took the seat by election, setting aside the named heir Peansith Rae. Wansen was murdered by Rend Greall at Themoor. Mound Sith took the seat by election. Greimfourn Goul challenged Mound Sith, won, and killed him at Themoor, taking the seat in 51.

Seven people were cast out: three for conspiracy, two for losing a claim, one for the losing side of a coup, and one for the losing side of an open challenge. Five people were murdered from within. Fifteen people were courted away from the ruler. The League fought one battle, won one war, and took one place. The period ended in 51 with Greimfourn Goul still holding the seat.

### House Buldbei, 43–43

The period of 43 marked the final collapse of House Buldbei, ending its rule through the mechanism of strongest succession. The house’s standing ended abruptly as it was defeated by the Galweall League, a power that succeeded by election.

In 43, the Galweall League and House Buldbei made peace following the collapse of House Buldbei. This settlement concluded the hostilities that had defined the end of the house’s existence.

Greimfourn Goul took the seat of the Galweall League in 51 by defeating Mound Sith in an open challenge. The previous ruler, Mound Sith, was killed by Goul at Themoor later that same year.
