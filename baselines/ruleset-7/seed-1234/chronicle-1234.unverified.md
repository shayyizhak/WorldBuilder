## The Trostead Compact, 2–21

- `partition-sum` — “Six people held the seat in total; three were killed, one cast out, one died, and one remained” — says 6 in total, but the parts add to 5

The Trostead Compact spent twenty years defined by a fragile alliance with House Buldbei that collapsed into war, followed by a descent into internal decay and external loss. The period began with the Compact binding itself to House Buldbei through three marriages in the first six years, while simultaneously occupying Geirnpei Shafts. This stability was short-lived. The Compact’s standing eroded through a series of defeats and rebellions, ending with the loss of two key places and a shift in leadership to Vun Doultheam, who held the seat as the period closed.

Faellwil Na ruled until killed by Lald Neirntean in year 4, after which Neinos Del took the seat by primogeniture, rejecting the claim of Frernli Peimeath. Neinos Del died in year 5, and Neallseis Staend succeeded him. Neallseis’s rule ended in year 10 when Paepu Noth challenged him and won, casting Neallseis out. Paepu Noth ruled until killed by Lald Neirntean in year 17. Kesfound Thrael then took the seat by right of birth, ruling until killed by Lald Neirntean in year 20. Vun Doultheam took the seat in year 20 and held it at the end. Six people held the seat in total; three were killed, one cast out, one died, and one remained.

The Compact’s relationship with House Buldbei shifted from alliance to conflict. After declaring war on House Buldbei in year 9 for Pei Delve, the Compact broke its alliance with them. House Buldbei and its allies defeated the Compact at Pei Delve in year 9, killing 23, and again in year 10, killing 18. Peace was made in year 11 after two years of war. The Compact sent four raids, all carrying off plunder: two against Gaehollow and two against Themoor. It suffered ten raids; five were beaten off, four carried off plunder, and one took nothing. The Compact lost Geirnpei Shafts to the Galweall League in year 20 after being defeated there, and lost Pellweagate to the Rising of Pellweagate in year 16. Two places rose against the Compact, both because its standing had fallen to nothing. Eight years of hunger killed 209 people and drove 214 from their homes.
