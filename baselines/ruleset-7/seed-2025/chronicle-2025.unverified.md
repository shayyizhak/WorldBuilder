The shift in allegiance did not bring stability; instead, the League faced a relentless series of raids from the Waeslefell Compact, which struck Kremoor and Skomere repeatedly, while the League’s own retaliatory raids were largely unsuccessful.

The final blow came in year 34 when the Waeslefell Compact, having broken its alliance with the League, declared war. The Waeslefell Compact defeated the League at Skomere, killing 179, and took the settlement. The Deafil League was finished, its last four followers scattering, and the period ended in year 35 with peace between the former enemies. Throughout these years, the League suffered four raids, sent out eight raids (five of which were beaten off), and saw five people cast out, including three for losing claims or coups.
