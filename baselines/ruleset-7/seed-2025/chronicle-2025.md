### The Thea Seam War: the Waeslefell Compact against the Baesveireach Compact, 4–10

The war began in 4 when the Waeslefell Compact declared war on the Baesveireach Compact for control of Thea Seam. In that same year, the Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam, leaving 26 dead. The conflict continued at Thea Seam in 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9, with the Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeating the Waeslefell Compact each time. The casualties in these engagements were 20, 13, 11, 9, and 8 dead, respectively.

The fighting ended in 10 when the Waeslefell Compact and the Baesveireach Compact made peace after six years of war. Both sides were exhausted by the end of the conflict.

### The the Waeslefell Compact Aggression: the Waeslefell Compact against the Baesveireach Compact, 26–30

In 26, the Waeslefell Compact declared war on the Baesveireach Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam, with 36 dead. In 27, the Waeslefell Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Thea Seam, with 30 dead, and took the site from the Baesveireach Compact.

In 28, the Waeslefell Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Baesveireach, with 82 dead. In 29, Ground Raeskur had Steapall Faedild murdered at Baesveireach. Later that year, the Waeslefell Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Baesveireach, with 66 dead, and took the settlement from the Baesveireach Compact.

In 30, the Waeslefell Compact and the Baesveireach Compact made peace after 4 years, following the collapse of the Baesveireach Compact.

### The Waeslefell Compact, 2–21

The Waeslefell Compact’s twenty-year span from 2 to 21 was defined by a war it lost and a seat it cycled through four rulers. The period began with the Compact occupying Fa Cut, a site no one held, and binding itself to the Baesveireach Compact through the marriage of Vaell Foull and Freallball Fisu. This alliance lasted only a year before the Compact declared war on Baesveireach for Thea Seam, breaking its own bond. The resulting conflict saw the Compact lose six successive battles at Thea Seam, with casualties ranging from 26 down to 8 dead. The war ended in year 10 with both sides exhausted, but the Compact had already suffered famine at Fa Cut and raids on Waeslefell and Weisfaewick.

Keirn Heapound died in year 10, and Vaell Foull took the seat by right of birth. His rule was marked by internal collapse and external pressure. Grefraem Meheath attempted to murder him and was cast out, later declared an outlaw for conspiracy. Fa Cut rose against the Compact in year 13 as its standing fell to nothing. That same year, Vor Saern challenged Vaell Foull openly, won, and took the seat. Vaell Foull was cast out. Vor Saern ruled until his death in year 17, during which time the Compact raided Kremoor and Skomere, carrying off plunder, while its own settlements were repeatedly raided by the Deafil League and Baesveireach, though most attacks were beaten off.

Vaell Foull took the seat again in year 17 by right of birth after Vor Saern’s death. He held the seat until the end of the period. The Compact continued to raid Kremoor and Skomere, taking grain and killing people, while the Deafil League’s raids on its settlements were consistently beaten off. The period ended with Vaell Foull still holding the seat, having survived the loss of six battles, the death of two rulers, and the exile of one challenger, while the Compact’s territory had shrunk from its initial occupation of Fa Cut to a state of repeated defense against its neighbors.

### The Baesveireach Compact, 4–30

The Baesveireach Compact’s twenty-seven years were defined by a war with the Waeslefell Compact that began in year 4 and ended only with the Baesveireach Compact’s collapse in year 30. The conflict consumed ten years of fighting across ten battles, shifting control of two places, Thea Seam and Baesveireach, from the Baesveireach Compact to its rival. Internal instability accompanied the external pressure, with four rulers holding the seat in succession, two dying and two being killed, while a plague and famine killed 530 people and displaced 407 others.

The war started when the Waeslefell Compact declared war on the Baesveireach Compact for Thea Seam in year 4, breaking their alliance. The Baesveireach Compact and its allies defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam in years 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9, with casualties ranging from 8 to 26 dead. In year 6, the Baesveireach Compact raided Waeslefell, carrying off 30 grain and killing 59. Raids on Weisfaewick in year 7 and Fa Cut in year 8 were beaten off. Peace was made in year 10 after six years of war. During this period, Sildbeld Bouthwoun ruled until his death in year 13. Kramboul Dend took the seat by strongest claim, setting aside the named heir Fur Roundpoull, who was cast out. Hou Lae’s conspiracy against Sildbeld Bouthwoun was uncovered in year 9, leading to Hou Lae’s execution.

The Baesveireach Compact’s standing fell very low by year 16, when Thea Seam rose against it. Kramboul Dend died in year 18, and Bage Busba took the seat by strongest claim. A plague broke out at Baesveireach in year 21, killing 213 people, followed by 179 dead and 224 fleeing in year 22, and 114 dead and 143 fleeing in year 23. Steapall Faedild returned from exile in year 24. The Baesveireach Compact raided Fa Cut in year 25, carrying off 20 ore. The Waeslefell Compact declared war again in year 26 over a long-standing grievance. The Baesveireach Compact defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam in year 26, but lost at Thea Seam in year 27, losing Thea Seam. Steapall Faedild had Bage Busba murdered in year 28 and took the seat by strongest claim, setting aside the named heir Drourn Nethi, who was cast out.

The Waeslefell Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Baesveireach in year 28, killing 82, and raided Baesveireach, carrying off 55 grain and killing 10. Steapall Faedild was killed by Ground Raeskur in year 29. The Waeslefell Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Baesveireach in year 29, killing 66, and took Baesveireach. The Waeslefell Compact and the Baesveireach Compact made peace in year 30 after the collapse of the Baesveireach Compact.

### The Waeslefell Compact, 22–41

The Waeslefell Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by a violent succession crisis, a prolonged war with the Baesveireach Compact, and a final collapse of authority that left its ruler holding a fractured seat. The period began with Vaell Foull ruling by primogeniture, but his reign ended abruptly in 24 when Ground Raeskur had him murdered at Skomere. Ground Raeskur took the seat by right of birth, but his rule was immediately contested. Staellou Keillthae attempted to kill Ground Raeskur, and after a conspiracy was uncovered, Keillthae was cast out and declared an outlaw. Ground Raeskur’s grip on power was further weakened when Fur Roundpoull returned from exile and challenged him openly in 32. Ground Raeskur lost the challenge and was cast out, and Fur Roundpoull took the seat.

Fur Roundpoull’s rule was short. He died in 33, and Rontaen Sin contested the claim of the named heir Rearn Thill. Rontaen Sin won, setting aside Rearn Thill’s claim, but the Compact was divided: Rearn Thill carried off two places as Greater Baesveireach. Rontaen Sin’s rule ended in 36 when Nounmam Mith had him murdered at Waeslefell. Deththou Keis took the seat by right of birth, but Nounmam Mith challenged him and won in 37, taking the seat by coup and casting out Keis. Nounmam Mith held the seat at the end of the period. Throughout these years, the Compact fought five battles, losing one to the Baesveireach Compact at Thea Seam in 26, but winning four others against the Baesveireach Compact and the Deafil League.

The war with the Baesveireach Compact began in 26 over a long-standing grievance. The Baesveireach Compact defeated the Waeslefell Compact at Thea Seam, killing 36. The Waeslefell Compact reversed this in 27, defeating the Baesveireach Compact at Thea Seam and taking the site. In 29, the Compact defeated the Baesveireach Compact at Baesveireach, taking that site and raiding it for 55 grain. The war ended in 30 after the collapse of the Baesveireach Compact. The Compact also fought the Deafil League in 34, defeating them at Skomere and taking the site, which finished the Deafil League. Despite these victories, the Compact’s standing fell to nothing, and Thea Seam rose against it in 35. The Compact sent two raids, both carrying off plunder, and suffered nine raids, all beaten off. Hunger killed 23 people across six years. The period ended with Nounmam Mith holding the seat, but the Compact had lost two places and its authority was broken.

### Free Kremoor, 28–51

Free Kremoor spent its twenty-four years as a fragile entity, born from the Deafil League in 28 and sustained by a succession of exiles who returned to fill its ranks. Feinnind Keitrith held the seat from the beginning, ruling for nineteen years before his own attempt on Greandsam Kreir in 44 led to his casting out. The power remained unstable, passing through Nounmam Mith, who also fell by attempted murder in 49, before Til Saeld took the seat. Throughout this period, Free Kremoor’s external posture was uniformly defensive; all five raids it sent out against the Waeslefell Compact were beaten off, and it never secured a foothold beyond its borders.

The internal life of the state was defined by the return of seven exiles and the subsequent purges that followed their ambitions. Feinnind Keitrith began by absorbing Staellou Keillthae, Touthseand Gourses, Vamkei Dridrill, Ground Raeskur, and Sealdvern Geitoun. The first major rupture came in 37 when Ground Raeskur abandoned the seat for the Waeslefell Compact, prompting a failed raid on Waeslefell. Sealdvern Geitoun’s subsequent attempt on Feinnind Keitrith in 39 resulted in his casting out and later declaration as an outlaw, marking the first of three castings for attempted murder.

Feinnind Keitrith’s rule ended in 44 after his own failed attempt on Greandsam Kreir, allowing Nounmam Mith to take the seat by election. Nounmam’s tenure was brief, ending in 49 when he was cast out for attempting to kill Woum Teldlei. Til Saeld assumed the seat in 49 with no remaining claimant. The period concluded with Free Kremoor still holding the seat, having survived three leadership changes and five failed external raids, but having lost one key figure to the Waeslefell Compact and executed one of its own captains.

## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Til Saeld over Free Kremoor, 49–51

Til Saeld assumed the seat of Free Kremoor in 49, following the exile of Nounmam Mith. Mith had been cast out after an attempt on the life of Woum Teldlei failed and was traced to him. With no claimant remaining, Saeld was raised from Kremoor to take the position.

The period ended in 51 when a raid by Free Kremoor on Fa Cut was beaten off.

### The rule of Haeth Tha over the Waeslefell Compact, 51–51

Haeth Tha took the seat of the Waeslefell Compact in 51 after contesting the claim of Thraebaern Ramdem, the named heir. The succession followed primogeniture, but Haeth Tha’s challenge succeeded, setting aside Thraebaern Ramdem’s claim. Thraebaern Ramdem was cast out of the Waeslefell Compact as the losing party.

The period was brief, ending in 51 with Haeth Tha still holding the seat. Ronith Wasnur, the previous ruler, had died earlier that year in Waeslefell. During this same year, a raid by Free Kremoor on Fa Cut was beaten off.
