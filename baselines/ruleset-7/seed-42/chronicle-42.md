# A history of the Rethkeis Vale

Seed 42, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War for Threi Cut: the Griwick Compact against the Wurn League, 5–10

The Griwick Compact declared war on the Wurn League in year 5 for Threi Cut. The Wurn League defeated the Griwick Compact at Threi Cut in year 5, with 45 dead. In year 6, the Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Threi Cut, with 35 dead. The Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Threi Cut again in year 7, with 29 dead, and took Threi Cut from the Wurn League.

In year 8, the Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Laehiford, with 107 dead, and took Laehiford from the Wurn League. In year 9, the Griwick Compact defeated the Wurn League at Hadale, with 101 dead. In year 10, the Griwick Compact and the Wurn League made peace after 5 years, both sides exhausted.

### The War for Griwick: the Kebarrow Compact against the Griwick Compact, 27–29

The war began in 27 when the Kebarrow Compact declared it on the Griwick Compact over a long-standing grievance. The Griwick Compact defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Vea Lode in the same year, with 57 dead. The conflict continued into 28, where the Griwick Compact again defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Vea Lode, resulting in 48 dead. During this period, Sou Dra had Thold Valmaer murdered at Griwick.

The fighting lasted for two years. In 29, the Kebarrow Compact and the Griwick Compact made peace. Both sides were exhausted by the end of the conflict.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### The Kebarrow Compact, 2–21

The Kebarrow Compact’s tenure from year 2 to 21 was defined by internal instability and a cycle of retaliatory violence. The period began with a marriage binding the Compact to the Griwick Compact, but it ended with the seat held by Sou Dra after a series of five short, violent rules. The Compact suffered six raids and sent out eight, yet its own people murdered four of their rulers from within. The standing of the house fell from its initial position to "very low" by year 15, when Meigate rose against it, before the seat passed through four hands in rapid succession.

Reweld Wul took the seat in year 8 after Searn Sisrill was killed by Math Ham. His rule was marked by a conspiracy uncovered in year 9, which led to the execution of Thulgea Bu. Wul himself was murdered in year 14 by Witrou Breim, who had returned to service in year 8. Witrou Breim took the seat by election but lost it in year 15 to Pouldrir Ho, who challenged him openly. Witrou Breim was cast out and later declared outlaw after a conspiracy was uncovered. During this time, the Compact’s standing had fallen very low, allowing Meigate to rise against it.

Pouldrir Ho’s rule ended in year 20 when Turaer Danpa, who had returned to service in year 17, had him murdered. Turaer Danpa was killed by Sou Dra in year 21, who then took the seat by election. Thra Bround had challenged Sou Dra openly in year 21 and lost, resulting in his death. Throughout the period, the Compact faced raids from the Wurn League and the Griwick Compact, suffering losses at Kebarrow and Meigate. It sent raids to Laehiford and Threi Cut, some carrying off plunder, others beaten off or taking nothing. The period ended with Sou Dra holding the seat, having survived an open challenge.

### The Wurn League, 2–21

The Wurn League’s twenty-year span from 2 to 21 was defined by a rapid unraveling of its alliances and a catastrophic loss of territory to the Griwick Compact. The period began with the League holding Threi Cut and bound by six marriages to its neighbors, but ended with the League stripped of two key places, engaged in a five-year war, and ruled by a successor who inherited a fractured position. The defining feature of the era was the conflict with the Griwick Compact, which resulted in five battles, all but one lost by the League, and the permanent loss of Threi Cut and Laehiford.

At the start, the League secured its position by occupying Threi Cut in 2 and weaving a dense network of marital ties. Witrou Breim married Beirn Naem in 3, linking the League to the Griwick Compact, while Teapeall Heampim married Thra Bround in the same year, binding the League to the Kebarrow Compact. Further marriages to the Griwick Compact followed in 4, and a marriage to the Kebarrow Compact in 5. These unions were tested early when the Kebarrow Compact raided Laehiford in 4 and Threi Cut in 5, carrying off grain and killing residents. The League retaliated by raiding Kebarrow in 5, killing 27 but taking nothing, and raiding Meigate in 6, killing 30 but taking nothing. The Griwick Compact responded to the League’s aggression by declaring war in 5 and breaking its alliance, then defeating the League at Threi Cut in 6 and 7, taking the site in 7. The League also lost Laehiford to the Griwick Compact in 8 after a defeat there.

Internal instability compounded the external losses. Reweld Wul was cast out in 7 for an attempted murder, allowing Math Ham to seize the seat by setting aside the claim of the named heir Witrou Breim. Math Ham’s rule was marked by violence and further defeat; he ordered the murder of Searn Sisrill at Kebarrow in 8 and was himself killed by Kei Leassel at Hadale in 10. The war with the Griwick Compact lasted five years, ending in peace in 10. Trem Lolkoll took the seat in 10 by right of birth and held it until the end of the period. The League’s raids in the later years, against Vea Lode, Threi Cut, Laehiford, and Griwick, were consistently beaten off or took nothing, reflecting its diminished power. Nael War returned from exile in 18, and a final marriage to the Kebarrow Compact occurred in 20, but the League had lost its territorial gains and its dominant standing.

### The Kebarrow Compact, 22–40

The Kebarrow Compact’s nineteen-year span from 22 to 40 was defined by a relentless decline from a position of electoral authority to total dissolution. The period began with Sou Dra holding the seat, a ruler whose tenure was marked by internal conspiracies and external aggression. By the end, the power was finished, its land seized and its followers scattered, having lost every battle it fought against the Griwick Compact. The seat passed through five holders, with four cast out and one killed, illustrating a rapid and violent erosion of stability.

Sou Dra’s rule was undermined by internal treachery and external war. In 25, a raid on Griwick was beaten off, and a conspiracy by Turaer Danpa was uncovered, leading to Danpa’s execution. The Compact’s aggression continued in 26 with a successful raid on Laehiford, which carried off 44 grain and killed 25, but this provoked a retaliatory raid on Kebarrow by the Griwick Compact in 26, which took 47 grain and killed 23. In 27, the Kebarrow Compact declared war on the Griwick Compact over a long-standing grievance. The war brought two defeats at Vea Lode: in 27, 57 were killed, and in 28, 48 were killed. Sou Dra’s authority further fractured as he ordered the murder of Thold Valmaer at Griwick in 28 and survived a conspiracy by Theald Va, who was executed.

The leadership of the Compact changed hands through open challenges and coups. In 29, Tor Nathgoull challenged Sou Dra openly, won, and took the seat, casting Sou Dra out. Tor Nathgoull’s rule was short-lived; in 32, Throll Kell challenged him, won, and took the seat by coup, casting Tor Nathgoull out. Throll Kell held the seat until 35, when Beas Krouthea challenged him openly, won, and took the seat, casting Throll Kell out. During this time, the Compact suffered a raid on Hadale in 35 that was beaten off. Beas Krouthea ordered the murder of Kondruth Tru at Hadale in 37, but was himself murdered by Kou Peis at Kebarrow in 38.

The final phase of the Compact saw it destroyed by the Griwick Compact. In 38, the Griwick Compact declared war on the Kebarrow Compact. The Griwick Compact and its allies defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Kebarrow in 38, killing 142, and took the seat by election to Diweith Mound. Diweith Mound attempted to murder Durnrin Drar in 39, failed, and was cast out. The Griwick Compact and its allies defeated the Kebarrow Compact at Kebarrow again in 39, killing 118, and took Kebarrow from the Compact. The Kebarrow Compact was finished, landless, with its last 9 followers scattering. The Griwick Compact and the Kebarrow Compact made peace in 40, after the collapse of the Kebarrow Compact.

### The Wurn League, 22–35

The Wurn League’s fourteen-year span from 22 to 35 was defined by internal fragmentation and external defeat. The period began with Trem Lolkoll holding the seat, but his rule was immediately undermined by conspiracy. Befu Seirn’s plot against him was uncovered in 22, leading to Seirn’s execution. This instability marked the start of a cycle where the seat changed hands eight times among nine rulers, with an average tenure of just 1.3 years. The League’s cohesion eroded through repeated purges and challenges, while its external relations deteriorated from alliance to war.

Internal violence and shifting alliances characterized the middle years. Trem Lolkoll was cast out in 26 after losing an open challenge to Gatros Hearn, who was himself killed by Sothkel Sald later that year. Sothkel Sald took the seat via coup but was killed by Saern Meastouth, who also seized power through coup. Saern Meastouth’s rule ended in 27 when he was cast out for an attempted murder. Teapeall Heampim took the seat by right of birth and died in 28. Paernrom Sir then contested the claim of the named heir Bu Rumpirn; primogeniture decided in Paernrom Sir’s favor, and Bu Rumpirn was cast out. Paernrom Sir was murdered in 30 by Stonand Ker, who took the seat by coup. Heillvar Maer succeeded by right of birth but was cast out in 32 for attempted murder, allowing Drarka Draernthun to take the seat by right of birth.

The League’s end came through war with the Griwick Compact. After a raid on Hadale in 24, the Griwick Compact declared war in 33 and broke its alliance with the League. The League suffered two defeats at Hadale, in 33 with 58 dead and in 34 with 87 dead. In 34, the Griwick Compact took Hadale from the Wurn League, and the League was finished, its last 25 followers scattering. Stonand Ker, who had taken the seat in 33, saw his house end with the power. The period closed with peace between the Griwick Compact and the remnants of the Wurn League in 35.

### The Meigate Covenant, 29–51

The Meigate Covenant’s twenty-three years from 29 to 51 were defined by a shift from isolation to entanglement, marked by a succession of three rulers and a persistent, ultimately fatal, conflict with the Griwick Compact. The period began with the Covenant’s separation from the Kebarrow Compact and its initial expansion through marriage and occupation. It ended with the Covenant still holding the seat under Drarka Draernthun, but having lost two battles to Griwick, suffered internal rebellions, and endured a famine that killed 61 people. The Covenant’s standing fell from nothing to nothing again, as it rose against Vea Lode only to see that place rise against it.

Raes Go took the seat in 29 when Meigate broke from the Kebarrow Compact. He bound the Meigate Covenant to the Vea Lode Covenant in 30 by marrying Mothgall Vafarn. The new power tested its strength immediately, sending raids on Griwick in 31 and Laehiford in 33, both of which were beaten off. Raes Go’s rule was challenged openly in 43 by Heillvar Maer, who had returned from exile in 33. Raes Go kept the seat, and Heillvar was cast out. In 44, Diweith Mound, who had returned from exile in 41, challenged Raes Go openly and won, taking the seat. Raes Go was cast out. Diweith’s rule was short; he was killed by Stonand Ker at Meigate in 46.

Drarka Draernthun took the seat in 46 after setting aside the claim of the named heir Tor Nathgoull. Drarka had married Thurnean Kourn in 35, binding the Meigate Covenant to the Griwick Compact, but this alliance broke in 47 when the Covenant declared war on Griwick over a long-standing grievance. The Covenant raided Griwick in 47, carrying off 23 grain and killing 16, but was defeated by the Griwick Compact at Threi Cut later that year, with 12 dead. The Griwick Compact defeated the Meigate Covenant again at Threi Cut in 48, with 10 dead. The war lasted two years, ending in peace in 49.

The Covenant’s internal stability eroded alongside its military fortunes. In 41, it occupied Vea Lode, which no one held. In 42, the Griwick Compact raided Vea Lode, carrying off 33 ore and killing 2. In 45, Vea Lode rose against the Meigate Covenant, whose standing had fallen to nothing. The Covenant also suffered a famine at Meigate in 38, where 61 died and grain was short by 7. Three people were cast out during this period: two for losing open challenges and one for losing a claim. Eleven exiles returned to take service. The Covenant sent eight raids; four were beaten off, and four carried off plunder. It suffered two raids, one of which took plunder and one of which took nothing. The period ended with Drarka Draernthun still holding the seat, but the Covenant had lost two battles to the Griwick Compact and seen its standing collapse.

## Reigns

_In the order they begin._

### The rule of Drarka Draernthun over the Meigate Covenant, 46–51

Drarka Draernthun took the seat of the Meigate Covenant in 46 after winning Kaes Rou away from the previous ruler and contesting the claim of the named heir Tor Nathgoull in an election. The previous ruler, Diweith Mound, had been killed by Stonand Ker at Meigate earlier that year. Drarka set aside Tor’s claim and cast him out. The period ran from 46 to 51, during which two people held the seat. Drarka remained in power until the end of the period.

The Meigate Covenant declared war on the Griwick Compact in 47 over a long-standing grievance. The alliance with the Griwick Compact broke down, and trade ceased. The Meigate Covenant raided Griwick in 47, carrying off 23 grain and killing 16. The Griwick Compact defeated the Meigate Covenant at Threi Cut in 47, with 12 dead, and again in 48, with 10 dead. The war lasted two years, ending in peace in 49. The Meigate Covenant sent three raids: one to Griwick in 47, one to Laehiford in 49 which was beaten off, and one to Hadale in 51, carrying off 49 grain and killing 14. Five exiles returned to service during this time. Hunger at Vea Lode lasted three years, killing two people. One person was courted away from Drarka in 46.

A marriage between Hehum Skul and Ta Poveil in 50 bound the Meigate Covenant to the Griwick Compact. Drarka’s child Threarnvi Teas was born in 50. The period ended in 51 with Drarka Draernthun still holding the seat.

### The rule of Paernmel Has over the Griwick Compact, 50–51

In 50, Paernmel Has challenged Raes Go openly for the Griwick Compact and won the seat. He killed Raes Go at Griwick later that year. Tor Nathgoull was raised to steward of the Griwick Compact in 50.

In 51, Visal Veill married Treild Haen at Hadale. Paernmel Has’s child, Gim Raeldrol, was born at Kebarrow. Voudreirn Wer, Trurkeand Busi, and Ta Poveil each won Bakrei Dea away from the ruler of the Griwick Compact. The Griwick Compact spent 205 silver buying goodwill for the fifth time and was not believed. The Meigate Covenant raided Hadale, carrying off 49 grain and killing 14.
