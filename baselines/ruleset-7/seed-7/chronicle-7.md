# A history of the Rulllound Vale

Seed 7, years 1–51. Every name, date and figure below is drawn from the simulation itself.


## Wars

_In the order they begin._

### The War of the Goummeidale Compact's Grudge: the Goummeidale Compact against House Thream, 5–7

In year 5, the Goummeidale Compact declared war on House Thream over the site of Sti Seam. The conflict began with a decisive engagement at that location, where the Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream, leaving 34 dead. The fighting continued at Sti Seam in year 6, resulting in another defeat for House Thream and 28 dead. Following this second loss, the Goummeidale Compact took Sti Seam from House Thream.

The war lasted for two years. In year 7, the Goummeidale Compact and House Thream made peace. Both sides were exhausted by the end of the conflict.

### The Long Quarrel over Kraeford: the Goummeidale Compact against the Kraeford Compact, 21–23

The Goummeidale Compact declared war on the Kraeford Compact in 21 over a long-standing grievance. In that same year, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Seithfaehollow, where 110 people died, and took the settlement from the Kraeford Compact.

In 22, the Goummeidale Compact defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, where 181 people died, and took the settlement from the Kraeford Compact.

In 23, the Goummeidale Compact and the Kraeford Compact made peace after two years, following the collapse of the Kraeford Compact.


## Powers

_In the order they begin._

### House Thream, 2–29

House Thream’s twenty-eight years ended in total destruction, a trajectory defined by a single, escalating conflict with the Goummeidale Compact. The period began with an alliance between the two powers, but that bond dissolved quickly into war. House Thream fought four battles against the Goummeidale Compact and lost all of them, losing two key settlements in the process. The house’s internal stability was equally fragile; six people held the seat in this period, with rules ending through death, murder, or the end of the house itself. The power that started with an alliance and a raiding capability finished landless, its followers scattered.

At the start, Freamteas Kraeth ruled House Thream until his death in year 3. His successor, Bran Nas, took the seat by the strongest claim. The alliance with the Goummeidale Compact, sealed by the marriage of Kokaes Threll to Thral Rei in year 3, broke in year 5 when the Compact declared war over a famine at Sti Seam. House Thream responded by raiding Seithfaehollow, carrying off 27 grain and killing 35 people. This raid was the only one that succeeded; the Goummeidale Compact defeated House Thream at Sti Seam in year 5 and again in year 6, taking Sti Seam from them. Bran Nas died in year 6, and Kokaes Threll took the seat by setting aside the claim of the named heir Ma Graltaeld, who was cast out.

Kokaes Threll’s rule was short. In year 8, Fous Bo had him murdered at Loufell. Vinbeil Real took the seat by the strongest claim. Fous Bo’s conspiracy against Vinbeil was uncovered in year 10, and Fous Bo was cast out. Vinbeil died in year 17, and Sen Tom took the seat. Sen Tom’s rule ended in year 26 when Maethhol Gondrur challenged him openly, won, and took the seat. Maethhol Gondrur killed Sen Tom at Loufell. The Goummeidale Compact declared war again in year 27 over a long-standing grievance. They defeated House Thream at Loufell in year 27 and again in year 28, taking Loufell from them. In year 28, House Thream was finished, its land taken and its last three followers scattering.

### The Goummeidale Compact, 22–41

The Goummeidale Compact’s twenty-year span from 22 to 41 was defined by violent succession and the elimination of external rivals. The period began with the murder of Freindmald Beindlea by Waeld Dould, a act that triggered a cascade of internal purges and external conquests. The Compact emerged from the destruction of the Kraeford Compact and House Thream, taking their seats at Kraeford and Loufell. Yet its internal stability remained fragile, marked by six internal murders and five castings out. The era closed with Maethhol Gondrur holding the seat, having inherited it after the murder of Brethneld Draer, while a plague at Seithfaehollow claimed 106 lives.

The Compact’s early years were shaped by the collapse of its neighbors. In 22, it defeated the Kraeford Compact at Kraeford, killing 181 and taking the settlement, which finished the Kraeford Compact. It also defeated House Thream at Loufell in 27 and 28, killing 123 and 103 respectively, and took Loufell from them, finishing House Thream. These victories were accompanied by one raid on Loufell in 24 that took nothing, and three raids suffered that were all beaten off. The Compact declared war on House Thream in 27, a conflict that lasted three years.

Succession within the Compact was unstable. Frouldtoun Dror took the seat in 22 by right of birth but was cast out in 28 for an attempted murder. He was succeeded by Voumfaern Ki, who was cast out in 29 after losing an open challenge to Skullhein Threill. Skullhein was murdered in 30, leading to Theirnpae Gro taking the seat, who was himself killed in 31 by Rateal Fototh after a coup. Rateal was then cast out in 31 by Vasgou Raend, who died in 34. Wold Risla took the seat in 34 by right of birth, was cast out in 35 after losing an open challenge to Brethneld Draer, who was murdered in 40. Maethhol Gondrur took the seat in 40 by right of birth. Six people were courted away from its rulers during this time.
